# Task: does the gold price reshape local politics in municipalities that have gold? Reduced forms, yes/no measure (explore, idea 0008)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0008_gold_politics.py`, on the exploration
half. It estimates idea 0008's politics reduced forms for nine pre-listed
outcomes, with their event study, legal-regime split,
Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment and the inference fixed below. Its
`results.json` carries `estimates.finite`, the field step s10's kill rule
reads, and every estimate. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0008-gold-ballot-yes-no/campaign.md`, step s9. Step
s10 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0008 (`research/ideas/0008-gold-ballot-yes-no.md`); decisions 0003 and
0011. The estimation code follows campaign 0007 step s14's script,
`explore/0007_gold_first_stage.py`: the same alternating projections,
clustered and Conley errors, and within-state randomisation inference.
Import nothing from it; copy what is needed. This is an **explore** task
(label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

## In scope

- `explore/0008_gold_politics.py` (new).
- `tests/test_explore_gold_yes_no_politics.py` (new).

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store,
  national=True)`, then `restrict` on `cd_ibge` before anything else.
- **Data read (referee R2).** It reads only:
  - `politics_panel` (derived), elections 2000-2024. These outcome years
    are the point of this step: the campaign lists them.
  - `gold_exposure` (both files), price years 1996-2023.
  
  It reads no MapBiomas, no `mining_panel` and nothing in `sealed/`.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** exactly the outcomes and
  blocks below; no other window, lag, exposure or subgroup.
- **Compute:** `numpy` and `polars` only; well inside 180 minutes.

### The design (fixed)

- **Unit:** municipality m × election e ∈ {2000, 2004, ..., 2024}.
- **Exposure:** E_m = `geo_exposed` (1 if the Geological Survey records any
  gold occurrence in the municipality, else 0), as a float.
- **Shock:** P̄_e, the mean of `ln_gold_real` over years e−4..e−1.
- **Regressor:** D_me = E_m × P̄_e.
- **Fixed effects:** municipality, and state × election.
- **Controls:** ln(`area_km2`) and `indigenous_share` (from `gold_exposure`),
  each × election dummies. `native_share_2004` is not used here: it is in
  `mining_panel`, which this script does not read.
- **The outcome family (nine, in this order),** each on its own available
  elections:
  1. `cands_per_seat`
  2. `mining_cand_share`
  3. `enc_mayor`
  4. `margin_mayor`
  5. `seat_turnover` (2004-2024)
  6. `mining_councillor_share`
  7. `incumbent_reelected` (2004-2024)
  8. `mining_money_share` (2004, 2008, 2012), estimated only if **at least
     two** of these elections have at least 1,000 exploration-half receipts
     with a CNAE (`n_receipts_with_cnae` summed).
     - Otherwise write `money.skipped: true` and the counts, and leave
       outcome 8 out of the family.
     - This rule is fixed now, before any run.
  9. `homicide_rate`
- **For each outcome:**
  - β and `se_cluster` (municipality, with the small-sample factor of 0007's s14);
  - Conley standard errors at 50, 100 and 200 km (uniform kernel, all
    elections, as in 0007's s14);
  - `ri_p`: within-state permutations of E_m, 1,000 draws, seed 20261111,
    `(1 + #{|β_draw| ≥ |β|}) / 1001`;
  - the outcome's exploration-half mean and SD, for magnitudes.
- **Benjamini-Hochberg:** on the RI p-values across the outcomes in the
  family (eight or nine), giving `q_bh` for each.
- **Event study:** E_m × election dummies, base 2000 (for outcomes 5 and 7,
  base 2004), with clustered standard errors.
- **Placebos, per outcome:**
  - E_m × the mean `ln_soy_real` over e−4..e−1;
  - `title_sand_pre2000_share` × P̄_e.
- **Robustness, per outcome:** E_m = `geo_gold_per_km2` (the density), and
  E_m = `title_gold_pre2000_share` (active titles only).
- **Legal regimes (secondary; report, do not claim):**
  - D_me × 1[e ≤ 2012] and D_me × 1[e ≥ 2016]. The 2016-2024 terms fall in
    the 2013-2023 "good faith" period.
  - No full term after the 2023 suspension exists, so none is estimated.
    Say so in `summary.md`.
  - Report both coefficients and their difference, with clustered
    standard errors.
- **Legal access (secondary, per outcome):** add D_me ×
  `geo_gold_indigenous_share` (0 where `geo_n_gold` is 0), as in 0007's s14, and
  report the interaction.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

For each outcome name `<o>` (the column names above):

- `outcomes.<o>.beta`, `.se`, `.conley_se_50km`, `.conley_se_100km`,
  `.conley_se_200km`, `.ri_p`, `.q_bh`;
- `.mean`, `.sd`, `.n_obs`, `.n_munis`;
- `.event.coef` and `.event.se` (objects keyed `e2004` ... `e2024`, base
  omitted);
- `.placebo.soy_beta`, `.soy_se`, `.sand_beta`, `.sand_se`;
- `.robust.density_beta`, `.density_se`, `.titles_beta`, `.titles_se`;
- `.regimes.pre_beta`, `.pre_se`, `.goodfaith_beta`, `.goodfaith_se`,
  `.diff_beta`, `.diff_se`;
- `.access.beta`, `.access.se`.

Also:

- `money.skipped` (bool) and `money.n_with_cnae` (object `e2004`,
  `e2008`, `e2012`);
- `family.n_outcomes` (int);
- `estimates.finite` (bool, **kill rule k1**): true only if every number
  above was finite when computed.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` (standard JSON, as in 0007's s14), and
they set `estimates.finite` to false. When outcome 8 is skipped, its keys
are absent, and that does not set the flag to false.

`summary.md`:

- one table: outcome, β, clustered SE, RI p, q, mean;
- a line that the clustered errors can over-reject with one common shock
  (Moreno-Louzada, Figueira and Picchetti 2025), so RI p and q come first;
- a line that nothing on the exploration half is a finding.

## Out of scope

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test recovers a planted β on one outcome
   within 10%, and a null outcome gets RI p above 0.05 for the fixed seed:
   - 60 municipalities in 3 states, seven elections;
   - one outcome with a planted effect, one null.
2. Benjamini-Hochberg matches a hand computation on five fixed p-values.
3. The money rule:
   - skips outcome 8 when fewer than two elections reach 1,000 receipts;
   - keeps it when two do;
   - `family.n_outcomes` follows.
4. `results.json` has `estimates.finite` and parses as standard JSON (no
   `NaN` or `Infinity`).
5. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before estimation (test).
6. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
