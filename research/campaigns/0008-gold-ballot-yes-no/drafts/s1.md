# Task: first stage with the yes/no gold measure: does the gold price move garimpo in municipalities that have gold? (explore, idea 0008)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0008_gold_yes_no_first_stage.py`, on the
exploration half. It estimates idea 0008's first stage with the yes/no gold
measure, its event study and placebos, and some report-only checks. Its
`results.json` carries the three fields that step s2's kill rules read.
Campaign `research/campaigns/0008-gold-ballot-yes-no/campaign.md`, step s1.
Step s2 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0008 (`research/ideas/0008-gold-ballot-yes-no.md`); decisions 0003 and
0011. The measure was chosen after campaign 0007's run
`research/explore-runs/0007/43438404852.md` showed its F (12.28, key
`robust.binary`). The estimation code is the `robust.binary` block of
campaign 0007 step s14's script, `explore/0007_gold_first_stage.py`: the
same panel, alternating projections, controls, clustered and Conley errors,
and within-state randomisation inference. **Copy what is needed; import
nothing from that script.** This is an **explore** task (label `explore`):
it may change only the two files below.

## In scope

- `explore/0008_gold_yes_no_first_stage.py` (new).
- `tests/test_explore_gold_yes_no_first_stage.py` (new).

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store,
  national=True)`, then `restrict` every table that has `cd_ibge` on
  `cd_ibge` before any other use of it. `gold_prices` is national and has
  no `cd_ibge`, so it is read without `restrict` (as in 0007's script).
- **Data read (referee R2).** It reads only:
  - `mining_panel` (derived, exploration rows), outcome years 1990-2024;
  - `gold_exposure` (both files): `gold_exposure.parquet` and
    `gold_prices.parquet`, price years 1989-2023 (the t-1 lag).

  It reads no politics or election data, no SIM data and nothing in
  `sealed/`.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** only the estimates and keys
  listed below; no other outcome, window, exposure or subgroup.
- **Compute:** `numpy` and `polars` only, as in 0007's script. The run
  must finish well inside `explore-run`'s 180 minutes.

### The design (fixed)

- **Unit:** municipality m × year t, t = 1990..2024.
- **Sample:** exactly as 0007's script builds it (`build_panel`):
  exploration-half municipalities with `n_cells` ≥ 1 and non-null controls
  and exposure columns, a balanced panel. Report the municipalities dropped
  and why.
- **Exposure:** D_m = `geo_exposed` from `gold_exposure` (1 if the
  Geological Survey records any gold occurrence in the municipality, else
  0), as a float.
- **Shock:** `ln_gold_real` of year t-1 (`gold_prices`).
- **Main outcome:** `garimpo_share` (0 to 1).
- **Regressor:** D_m × ln_gold_real_{t-1}.
- **Fixed effects:** municipality, and state × year (`uf` from
  `gold_exposure`).
- **Controls:** `native_share_2004` (mining_panel), ln(`area_km2`) and
  `indigenous_share`, each × a full set of year dummies (one dropped),
  partialled out as in 0007's script.
- **Inference:**
  - `se`: municipality-clustered, small-sample factor
    G/(G-1) × (N-1)/(N-K), as in 0007's script;
  - `f_cluster = (beta / se)²`;
  - `ri_p`: 1,000 draws, **seed 20261113**, each permuting D_m across
    municipalities within the same state and re-estimating beta with
    everything else fixed; `ri_p = (1 + #{|b_draw| ≥ |b|}) / 1001`;
  - Conley errors at 50, 100 and 200 km, uniform kernel over municipality
    scores, as in 0007's script.
- **Event study:** D_m × 1[year = y] for y = 1990..2024 except 2000 (the
  base), same fixed effects and controls, clustered errors.
  `placebo.lead_ratio = |mean(b_y, y in 1990..2001, y ≠ 2000)| /
  |mean(b_y, y in 2002..2024)|`. If the denominator is 0, write
  1000000000.0 (the sentinel of 0007's script); the kill rule then fires.

### Report-only blocks (no kill rule reads them)

1. **Secondary outcomes with D,** same design, each with beta, `se` and
   `ri_p` (same seed): `garimpo_gold_share`, `garimpo_other_share`,
   `industrial_gold_share` (each `<x>_ha / (n_cells × 100)`), and
   `ln_garimpo_ha` = ln(1 + `garimpo_ha`), as in 0007's script.
2. **Placebos** (main outcome), as in 0007 s14:
   - price: D_m × `ln_soy_real` (t-1), in place of gold;
   - exposure: `title_sand_pre2000_share` × `ln_gold_real` (t-1).
3. **Why the density measure failed.** With E_m = `geo_gold_per_km2`:
   - the products a_m = |E_m × (garimpo_share_m,2007 − garimpo_share_m,2006)|
     for every sample municipality;
   - `density.top5`: the five municipalities with the largest a_m, largest
     first, each as an object with only `cd_ibge` and `uf`;
   - `density.top1_share`: the largest a_m over the sum of a_m across all
     sample municipalities;
   - `density.f_drop_top1` and `density.f_drop_top5`: the clustered F of
     the density first stage (E_m × ln_gold_real_{t-1}, otherwise the main
     design) without the top one, and without the top five, municipalities.
4. **How sturdy the yes/no result is** (main design, clustered F only):
   - `sturdy.loso_min_f` and `sturdy.loso_min_state`: the F leaving out
     each state in turn (all its municipalities); the smallest F and the
     `uf` of the state left out;
   - `sturdy.f_drop_top5_garimpo`: the F without the five municipalities
     with the largest `garimpo_share` in 2024;
   - `sturdy.f_1990_2012` and `sturdy.f_2013_2024`: the F on years
     1990-2012 only and on 2013-2024 only, with the fixed effects and
     control × year dummies rebuilt for those years.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

- `first_stage.beta`, `first_stage.se`, `first_stage.f_cluster` (**kill
  rule k1**), `first_stage.ri_p`, `first_stage.conley_se_50km`,
  `first_stage.conley_se_100km`, `first_stage.conley_se_200km`,
  `first_stage.n_obs`, `first_stage.n_munis`, `first_stage.n_states`,
  `first_stage.n_exposed` (municipalities with D_m = 1).
- `placebo.lead_ratio` (**kill rule k2**), `placebo.soy_beta`,
  `placebo.soy_se`, `placebo.sand_beta`, `placebo.sand_se`.
- `event.coef.y<year>` and `event.se.y<year>` for y1990 .. y2024, without
  y2000.
- `secondary.<outcome>.beta`, `.se`, `.ri_p` for `garimpo_gold_share`,
  `garimpo_other_share`, `industrial_gold_share` and `ln_garimpo_ha`.
- `density.top5` (list of objects with `cd_ibge`, `uf`),
  `density.top1_share`, `density.f_drop_top1`, `density.f_drop_top5`.
- `sturdy.loso_min_f`, `sturdy.loso_min_state`,
  `sturdy.f_drop_top5_garimpo`, `sturdy.f_1990_2012`,
  `sturdy.f_2013_2024`.
- `estimates.finite` (bool, **kill rule k3**): true only if every number
  above was finite when computed. A non-finite number is written as
  `null`, never as `NaN` or `Infinity`, so `results.json` stays standard
  JSON. The lead-ratio sentinel also sets this flag to false.

Nothing else.

`summary.md`:

- opens with the main estimate in one sentence;
- says plainly that the yes/no measure was chosen after seeing campaign
  0007's results (`research/explore-runs/0007/43438404852.md`), so nothing
  here is a finding;
- gives the event study as a table, each year's coefficient and SE next to
  that year's `ln_gold_real` (t-1);
- one line per report-only block; that clustered errors can over-reject
  with one common shock (Moreno-Louzada, Figueira and Picchetti 2025), so
  the RI p comes first.

## Done when

Each item is checked on data built inside the test, with no network or GCS.

1. A planted effect is recovered and a null gives a small F: on a
   synthetic panel (60 municipalities in 3 states, 1990-2024, D yes/no),
   outcome = 0.02 × D × lnP + municipality and state-year effects + noise
   gives beta within 10% and F above 10; with the outcome independent of
   D × lnP, F is below 10.
2. The randomisation p uses `(1 + #{|b_draw| ≥ |b|}) / 1001` (a test with
   a known set of draws).
3. `placebo.lead_ratio` matches a hand computation on fixed coefficients,
   and a zero denominator gives the sentinel and `estimates.finite` false.
4. Leave-one-state-out (minimum F and its state), the drop of the top five
   `garimpo_share` 2024 municipalities, and the density top-five and drop
   rules pick the right municipalities and states on a small toy panel.
5. `results.json` has every key listed above, including
   `first_stage.f_cluster`, `placebo.lead_ratio` and `estimates.finite`;
   no key contains a dot; a NaN is written as `null` and sets
   `estimates.finite` to false; the file parses with `json.loads` set to
   reject `NaN` and `Infinity`.
6. `restrict` is called before any other use of each table that has
   `cd_ibge`; rows outside the exploration half are dropped before
   estimation (test).
7. `tests/test_explore.py` passes (the script calls
   `exploration_municipalities(` with `national=True`, and `restrict(`).
8. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
