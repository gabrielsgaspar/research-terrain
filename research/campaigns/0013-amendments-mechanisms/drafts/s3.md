# Task: whose votes move with an allied mayor: the party, its sitting deputies or its state deputies, at campaign 0011's 7.5% window (explore, idea 0010)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0010_vote_mechanisms.py`, on the
exploration half. When a federal deputy's party narrowly wins a mayoralty,
it asks whose votes move at the next general election: the whole party's
federal-deputy vote, its sitting deputies' vote, or also its state
deputies' vote (they send no federal amendments). Its `results.json`
carries `placebo.t0_fail` and `estimates.finite`, the fields step s4's
kill rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0013-amendments-mechanisms/campaign.md`, step s3. Step
s4 runs it through `explore-run`.

### Facts about the data

`data/contracts/amendments_panel.md`. One row per (`cycle`, `deputy`,
`cd_ibge`) where the deputy's t0 party (`deputy_party`, a party number)
had a mayoral finalist; `margin` is that finalist's margin.

- `share_t0`, `share_t1`: the deputy's share of all federal-deputy votes
  in the municipality; `share_t1` and `d_share` are null where `ran_t1` is
  false.
- `party_share_t0`, `party_share_t1`: the party's (or its federation's)
  share of the federal-deputy vote in the municipality, nominal plus party
  list, followed through mergers; `state_party_share_*`: the same for state
  deputies. They do not depend on `ran_t1` and are the same for every
  deputy of one party in one municipality and cycle. In PR #436's dry run
  they had no nulls in A and B and 2 nulls at t0 in C.
- Exploration-half rows: A 16,533, B 12,830, C 17,067.

### Party level (the main analysis)

Collapse the panel to one row per (`cycle`, `cd_ibge`, `deputy_party`):

- `margin`, `win`, the four party shares: one value per group; raise if
  they differ within a group (shares by more than 1e-9).
- `d_party` = `party_share_t1` − `party_share_t0`.
- `d_inc` = the sum over the group's panel deputies of `share_t1` (0 where
  `ran_t1` is false) minus the sum of their `share_t0`.
- `d_state` = `state_party_share_t1` − `state_party_share_t0`.
- `party_minus_inc` = `d_party` − `d_inc`; `party_minus_state` =
  `d_party` − `d_state` (each one fit, so its error is the difference's).
- Fixed effects: state × party × cycle, written into a column `deputy`
  (for example `uf|deputy_party|cycle`).
- A race is (`cycle`, `cd_ibge`), as in `prepare`; draws are made once
  over the party-level table's races. Re-flipping a race flips the sign of
  the margin of every row in it.
- Count the races whose two rows have exactly equal `party_share_t1`
  (two finalist parties that are one party or federation at t1):
  `counts.races_equal_t1_share`.

For each outcome: pooled (`estimate`: b, se, 95% interval b ± 1.96 × se,
`ri_p`, mean, units, races), by cycle and C minus A (`by_cycle_fit`: b,
se, 95% interval), and the pooled estimate at 5% (b, se, `ri_p`). Rows with
a null outcome leave that outcome's fit.

### Deputy level

The main run's reduced form with the deputy who did not run counted as 0:
Y = (`share_t1` if `ran_t1` else 0) − `share_t0`, on every row, deputy
fixed effects (`deputy` is cycle-specific). Pooled with `ri_p`, by cycle
and C minus A. `deputy.n_not_running` = rows at h with `ran_t1` false.

### Descriptive only (labelled so in results and summary)

Among allied towns (`win` true, |margin| ≤ h, `ran_t1` true):
`pyfixest.feols` of `d_share` on `amend_post_log_pc` with fixed effects
`deputy` and town × party × cycle (`cd_ibge`, `deputy_party`, `cycle`),
`vcov={"CRV1": "cd_ibge"}`. Report b, se, rows used and
`"label": "descriptive"`. It is not a causal estimate: the money is not
randomised.

### Placebo

The party-level model at h on `party_share_t0` and on
`state_party_share_t0` (both fixed before the mayoral race). For each:
b, se, `ri_p`, the mean of Y on the fit's rows, and `ratio` = |b| / mean.
`placebo.t0_fail` = 1 if for either of the two |b| > 0.10 × its mean
**and** its `ri_p` < 0.05, else 0 (an integer).

### Declared estimates and Holm

Two, with randomisation p-values adjusted together by Holm's method:
`d_party` pooled and `party_minus_state` pooled. Everything else is
descriptive, and `summary.md` says so.

### Reading, declared before the run (for `summary.md`, word for word)

"If `d_state` is about as large as `d_party`, the allied mayor lifts the
whole ticket, which points to the mayor's campaigning. If `d_party` is
near 0 while `d_inc` is positive, votes are reshuffled toward sitting
deputies. Either way, compare with how the money moved across cycles
(step s1)."

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

`<scope>` is `pooled`, `A`, `B`, `C` or `c_minus_a`.

- `window.h_pct` (7.5), `window.check_pct` (5), `window.source`.
- `counts.party_rows_h`, `counts.races_h`, `counts.groups_h`,
  `counts.by_cycle.<A|B|C>.rows`, `.races`, `counts.races_equal_t1_share`.
- `party.<d_party|d_inc|d_state|party_minus_inc|party_minus_state>.<scope>`:
  pooled `b`, `se`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `ri_p`, `mean`, `n_units`,
  `n_races`; by cycle and `c_minus_a`: `b`, `se`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`.
- `check_5pct.<outcome>`: `b`, `se`, `ri_p`.
- `deputy.rf_zero.<scope>` (as above), `deputy.n_not_running`.
- `descriptive.dose`: `b`, `se`, `n_obs`, `label`.
- `placebo.party_t0`, `placebo.state_t0` (`b`, `se`, `ri_p`, `mean`,
  `ratio`), `placebo.t0_fail` (**k1**, 0 or 1).
- `declared.<d_party|party_minus_state>`: `p`, `p_holm`.
- `estimates.finite` (**k2**, bool): true only if every number above is
  finite.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite`
false.

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test (three cycles, at least 300 races,
   parties with several deputies per town) recovers planted jumps in
   `d_party`, `d_inc` and `d_state` within 10%, and `party_minus_state`
   equals the difference of the two.
2. The collapse: `d_inc` sums the group's deputies with 0 for one who did
   not run; differing `margin` within a group raises.
3. The deputy-level reduced form counts a deputy who did not run as
   0 − `share_t0` (test).
4. `t0_fail` is 1 on a synthetic panel with a planted jump in
   `party_share_t0` of 20% of its mean, and 0 with none.
5. Holm on (0.01, 0.04) gives (0.02, 0.04).
6. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5.
7. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is
   computed.
8. `results.json` carries `placebo.t0_fail` and `estimates.finite`, and
   parses as standard JSON; the two figures are written, each under
   400 KB.
9. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
