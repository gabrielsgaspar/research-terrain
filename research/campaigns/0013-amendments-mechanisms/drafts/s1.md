# Task: the instrument by payment type, in levels and in percent, with checks for sorting, at campaign 0011's 7.5% window (explore, idea 0010)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0010_instrument_sorting.py`, on the
exploration half. It answers three questions about the first stage of idea
0010 (a deputy's party narrowly wins a mayoralty, and the deputy sends more
amendment money there): is the growth by cycle more favouritism or just more
money (a Poisson premium in percent), does it run through Pix, and does it
survive controls for sorting. Its `results.json` carries
`first_stage.f_cluster`, `placebo.pre_fail` and `estimates.finite`, the
fields step s2's kill rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0013-amendments-mechanisms/campaign.md`, step s1. Step
s2 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0010 (`research/ideas/0010-amendments-share-of-quota.md`); decisions
0003, 0008 and 0011; campaign 0011's gate record
`research/explore-runs/0010/44484340952.md` (the window) and main run
`research/explore-runs/0010/44538371996.md`. This is an **explore** task
(label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

The linear estimator is the one of `explore/0010_amendments_main.py`. Load
that script with the `_load` pattern it uses for its own gate
(`importlib.util.spec_from_file_location`, registered in `sys.modules`
under a new name such as `main0010`) and reuse, unchanged (from it, or
through its `gate` and `gate.g9` modules where it does not re-export a name):

- `prepare` (adds `race`, 0..R-1 over sorted (`cycle`, `cd_ibge`));
- `sign_draws` (2,000 draws, seed 20261115), `n_races`, `in_window`,
  `_check_draws`;

  cycle dummies, A, B and C);
- `_all_finite`, `_clean`, `_paths`, `GRID`.

`deputy` is the t0 `sq_candidato`, so it is specific to a cycle: deputy
fixed effects are deputy-by-cycle fixed effects.

### Facts about the data

`data/contracts/amendments_panel.md`. One row per cycle, deputy and
municipality where the deputy's party had a mayoral finalist; `margin` is
that finalist's margin, `win` = `margin` > 0.

- Cycles: A (mayor 2016), B (2020), C (2024).
- `amend_post_total`, `amend_post_pix`: reais, nominal, ≥ 0 (each floored
  at 0 after summing). Pix (special transfers) exists from 2020, so it is
  0 in cycle A.
- `amend_post_log_pc` = ln(1 + `amend_post_total` / `population_m`); null
  where `population_m` is null or 0. Same for `amend_pre_log_pc`.
- `party_held_mayor` (bool): the deputy's party had the top candidacy at
  m-4. `fin_age` may be null.
- Exploration-half rows (v3 panel, manifest of 2026-11-20): A 16,533,
  B 12,830, C 17,067. At 7.5% the gate record has 2,300 races and 13,809
  rows.

### Measures (computed by the script)

For each instrument `<inst>`:

- `total` = `amend_post_total`;
- `pix` = `amend_post_pix`, cycles B and C only (cycle A rows are left out
  of every Pix fit, and no Pix key is written for A);
- `nonpix` = `amend_post_total` − `amend_post_pix`, floored at 0; count
  the rows floored (`counts.nonpix_floored`).

and three measures `<measure>`:

- `log_pc`: ln(1 + reais / `population_m`) (for `total` it must equal
  `amend_post_log_pc`; a test checks);
- `any`: 1 if reais > 0, else 0;
- `poisson`: reais, by Poisson (below).

Rows with `population_m` null or 0 leave every fit and are counted
(`counts.no_population`).

### The models (fixed)

- **Linear:** `estimate` at |margin| ≤ h: Y on win, margin and win ×
  margin, deputy fixed effects, clustered by `cd_ibge`, randomisation p
  from the race re-flips (2,000 draws over the races of all three cycles,
  drawn once). Report b, se, the 95% interval b ± 1.96 × se, `ri_p`, the
  mean of Y, units and races.
- **By cycle and differences (linear):** a function `cycle_fit(table, w,
  column, cycles)` that generalises `by_cycle_fit` to any list of cycles
  (each cycle's win, margin and win × margin, deputy fixed effects, the
  same clustered variance), returning b and se per cycle and of the
  difference of two cycles. On (A, B, C) it must give exactly
  `by_cycle_fit`'s numbers (test). Clustered errors only, no
  randomisation p.
- **Poisson:** `pyfixest.fepois` of reais on `win + margin + win:margin`
  with deputy fixed effects, `offset` ln(`population_m`), `vcov={"CRV1":
  "cd_ibge"}`, rows at |margin| ≤ h. Report b, se, the two-sided normal p
  (pyfixest's), the premium in percent 100 × (exp(b) − 1) and its 95%
  interval 100 × (exp(b ± 1.96 × se) − 1), and the rows used.
  - By cycle: a separate fit per cycle.
  - A difference between cycles (C minus A, C minus B): one fit on the two
    cycles' rows with every regressor also interacted with a dummy for the
    later cycle (`win:isC`, `margin:isC`, `win:margin:isC`); the
    difference is the coefficient of `win:isC`. Its point estimate equals
    the difference of the separate fits (test).
  - Pix minus non-Pix, cycles B and C pooled: stack the rows twice (Pix
    reais, non-Pix reais) with a dummy `is_pix`, fixed effects deputy ×
    type, every regressor also interacted with `is_pix`; the difference is
    the coefficient of `win:is_pix`. Its point estimate equals the pooled
    B+C Pix b minus the pooled B+C non-Pix b (test).
  - Fixed-effect groups that pyfixest drops (all-zero outcome) are
    dropped; the rows used are reported.

### What to estimate

**(a) Instruments.** For each `<inst>` and `<measure>`:

- pooled (all cycles; for `pix`, B and C);
- by cycle (A, B, C; for `pix`, B and C);
- `c_minus_a` for `total` and `nonpix`; `c_minus_b` for `pix`;
- at the 5% check window: the pooled estimate only.

**(b) Level decomposition.** The linear model on reais per inhabitant
(`reais / population_m`, not logged) for `total`, `pix` and `nonpix`, by
cycle (pix: B and C) and pooled B+C. `levels.pix_share.<B|C|pooled_bc>` =
b(pix) / b(total) on the same rows. `levels.gap.<scope>` = b(total) −
b(pix) − b(nonpix) (0 unless rows were floored).

**(c) Sorting**, for `total`, `log_pc`, pooled. The controls are
`party_held_mayor`, `amend_pre_log_pc`, `fin_female`, `fin_age`,
`fin_higher_ed`, `n_mayor_cands` and ln(`population_m`) (booleans as 0/1).

- The model with controls: the linear model plus the controls as extra
  regressors, demeaned within deputy like the rest; the clustered variance
  with K = 3 + the number of controls; randomisation p from the same race
  re-flips with the controls held fixed. With no controls it must give
  exactly `estimate`'s b, se and `ri_p` (test). Rows with a null control
  leave the fit and are counted (`sorting.controls.n_dropped`).
  `f_cluster` = (b / se)².
- `sorting.controls`: at h.
- `sorting.held_true`, `sorting.held_false`: rows with `party_held_mayor`
  true, false; the controls without `party_held_mayor`.
- `sorting.donut`: with controls, leaving out |margin| < 0.005.
- `sorting.windows.<w2_5pct|w5pct|w7_5pct|w10pct>`: with controls at
  2.5%, 5%, 7.5% and 10%.

**(d) Placebo.** The linear model (no controls) at h on
`amend_pre_log_pc`. `placebo.post_b` is (a)'s pooled `total` `log_pc` b.
`placebo.pre_fail` = 1 if |b_pre| > 0.5 × |b_post| **and** the placebo's
`ri_p` < 0.05, else 0 (an integer).

**Declared estimates and Holm.** Three, with p-values adjusted together
by Holm's method:

- `first_stage_controls`: (c)'s `sorting.controls`, its `ri_p`;
- `poisson_total_c_minus_a`: (a)'s Poisson `total` C minus A, its p;
- `poisson_pix_minus_nonpix`: the stacked Poisson difference, its p.

Everything else is descriptive, and `summary.md` says so.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

`<scope>` is `pooled`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `c_minus_a` or `c_minus_b`, as
listed above.

- `window.h_pct` (7.5), `window.check_pct` (5), `window.source` (the
  record path).
- `counts.units_h`, `counts.races_h`, `counts.by_cycle.<A|B|C>.units`,
  `.races`, `counts.no_population`, `counts.nonpix_floored`.
- `instruments.<inst>.<measure>.<scope>`:
  - linear, pooled: `b`, `se`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `ri_p`, `mean`,
    `n_units`, `n_races`; linear, by cycle and differences: `b`, `se`,
    `ci_low`, `ci_high`;
  - Poisson: `b`, `se`, `p`, `premium_pct`, `premium_ci_low`,
    `premium_ci_high`, `n_obs`.
- `check_5pct.<inst>.<measure>`: `b`, `se`, and `ri_p` (linear) or `p`
  (Poisson).
- `levels.<inst>.<scope>` (`b`, `se`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`),
  `levels.pix_share.<B|C|pooled_bc>`, `levels.gap.<A|B|C|pooled_bc>`.
- `sorting.controls`, `sorting.held_true`, `sorting.held_false`,
  `sorting.donut`, `sorting.windows.<w>`: each `b`, `se`, `ci_low`,
  `ci_high`, `ri_p`, `f_cluster`, `n_units`, `n_races`, `n_dropped`.
- `first_stage.f_cluster` (**k1**: the F of `sorting.controls`),
  `first_stage.b`, `first_stage.se`.
- `placebo.pre` (`b`, `se`, `ri_p`, `mean`), `placebo.post_b`,
  `placebo.ratio` (|b_pre| / |b_post|), `placebo.pre_fail` (**k2**, 0 or 1).
- `poisson_diff.pix_minus_nonpix`: `b`, `se`, `p`, `ratio_pct`
  (100 × (exp(b) − 1)), `n_obs`.
- `declared.<name>`: `p`, `p_holm`, `p_kind` (`"ri"` or `"cluster"`).
- `estimates.finite` (**k3**, bool): true only if every number above is
  finite.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite`
false. `results.json` must stay under 200 KB (`write_results` enforces
it).

### `summary.md`

The window and its source record; the declared estimates with raw and
Holm p; tables for (a), (b), (c) and (d); the caveats:

- the same exploration half and window campaign 0011 analysed, so this is
  a further look at the same data, and questions 1 to 3 were added after
  its results;
- national payment totals grew from cycle A to C, so a growing `log_pc`
  jump need not mean growing favouritism; the Poisson premium is the
  comparison in percent;
- by-cycle linear estimates have clustered errors only;
- nothing on the exploration half is a finding.

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test (three cycles, at least 300 races,
   deputies with several municipalities, Pix only in B and C) recovers a
   planted linear jump in `log_pc` within 10%, and a planted Poisson
   premium of 30% within 5 percentage points.
2. `cycle_fit` on (A, B, C) equals `by_cycle_fit`; the Poisson C minus A
   and Pix minus non-Pix point estimates equal the differences of the
   separate fits.
3. The controls model with no controls equals `estimate` (b, se, `ri_p`).
4. `pre_fail` is 1 on a synthetic panel with a planted pre-window jump
   larger than half the post jump, and 0 with none.
5. Holm: for p-values (0.01, 0.04, 0.03) the adjusted values are
   (0.03, 0.06, 0.06).
6. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5 (a
   temporary record).
7. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is
   computed.
8. `results.json` carries `first_stage.f_cluster`, `placebo.pre_fail` and
   `estimates.finite`, and parses as standard JSON; the three figures are
   written, each under 400 KB.
9. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
