# Task: when allied towns are paid: monthly amendment payments around the mayoral term, at campaign 0011's 7.5% window (explore, idea 0010)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0010_payment_timing.py`, on the exploration
half. It estimates, month by month from January of the year before the
mayoral election to September of the next general election, how much more
amendment money (reais per inhabitant) a deputy pays to a town where the
deputy's party narrowly won. Its `results.json` carries
`placebo.lead_ratio` and `estimates.finite`, the fields step s6's kill
rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0013-amendments-mechanisms/campaign.md`, step s5. Step
s6 runs it through `explore-run`.

### Facts about the data

- `data/contracts/amendments_monthly.md`: exactly the panel's (`cycle`,
  `deputy`, `cd_ibge`) rows times 46 months, January of m-1 to October of
  t1. `ym` is YYYYMM (int). `amend_total`, `amend_pix`: reais, nominal,
  ≥ 0, Pix ≤ total. Exploration-half rows: A 760,518, B 590,180,
  C 785,082 (16,533, 12,830 and 17,067 panel rows).
- October of t1 is dropped here: the months used are j = 0..44, January
  of m-1 (j = 0) to September of t1 (j = 44).
- Join on (`cycle`, `deputy`, `cd_ibge`) to the panel rows with |margin| ≤
  h. Raise if a monthly row has no panel row, or a panel row inside h does
  not have exactly 46 months. Rows with `population_m` null or 0 leave
  every fit and are counted (`counts.no_population`).

### Months

- j = 0..44; j = 12 × (year − (m − 1)) + (month − 1).
- Pre months: j = 0..20 (January m-1 to September m). Post months:
  j = 24..44 (January m+1 to September t1). j = 21..23 (October to
  December of m: the mayoral election to the inauguration) are neither.
- Marks for the figures: the mayoral election j = 21 (October m), the
  inauguration j = 24 (January m+1), the general election j = 45 (October
  t1, just past the last month), and in cycle C only the Supreme Court's
  Pix rulings of August 2024 (j = 19) and December 2024 (j = 23).

### The models (fixed)

All on panel rows with |margin| ≤ h, deputy fixed effects, errors
clustered by `cd_ibge` (`fe_fit`): b and se of `win`. No randomisation
inference (several hundred fits; the kill rule reads coefficients only).

- **Monthly:** for each j, Y = that month's reais per inhabitant
  (`amend_total` / `population_m`; `amend_pix` / `population_m` for Pix).
  `total`: by cycle (A, B, C) and pooled (all three; j aligned across
  cycles). `pix`: B, C and pooled B+C (`pooled`).
- **Half-years:** Y = the mean of the months' reais per inhabitant over
  the half-year, which equals the mean of the monthly b (same rows; a test
  checks): `y_m1_h1` (j 0-5), `y_m1_h2` (6-11), `y_m_h1` (12-17),
  `y_m_h2` (18-23), `y_p1_h1` (24-29), `y_p1_h2` (30-35), `y_t1_h1`
  (36-41), `y_t1_q3` (42-44). Same instruments and scopes.
- **Cumulative gap:** for each j, Y = the sum of reais per inhabitant over
  months 0..j; its b equals the running sum of the monthly b (test). Same
  instruments and scopes.
- **Lead ratio (k1):** from `total`, pooled: `placebo.pre_mean` = the mean
  of the monthly b over j = 0..20; `placebo.post_mean` = the mean over
  j = 24..44; `placebo.lead_ratio` = |pre_mean| / |post_mean| (the
  kill-rule library's `pretrend_ratio`).

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

`NN` is two digits, 00..44. `<scope>` is `A`, `B`, `C` or `pooled` for
`total`, and `B`, `C` or `pooled` for `pix`.

- `window.h_pct` (7.5), `window.source`.
- `counts.rows_h`, `counts.by_cycle.<A|B|C>.rows`, `.races`,
  `counts.no_population`, `counts.months` (45).
- `months.j<NN>`: `ym_A`, `ym_B`, `ym_C`, `phase` (`"pre"`,
  `"transition"` or `"post"`).
- `monthly.<total|pix>.<scope>.j<NN>`: `b`, `se`, `n_units`.
- `halves.<total|pix>.<scope>.<half>`: `b`, `se`.
- `cumulative.<total|pix>.<scope>.j<NN>`: `b`, `se`.
- `placebo.pre_mean`, `placebo.post_mean`, `placebo.lead_ratio` (**k1**).
- `estimates.finite` (**k2**, bool): true only if every number above is
  finite.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite`
false. Keep each block to the keys listed: `results.json` must stay under
200 KB (`write_results` enforces it).

## Done when

1. On a synthetic panel and monthly table built in the test (three
   cycles, at least 300 races), a jump planted only in month j = 30 gives
   b(j30) near the jump and b(j10) near 0.
2. The half-year b equals the mean of its monthly b, and the cumulative b
   equals the running sum (to 1e-9).
3. `lead_ratio` is above 0.5 when pre months carry a planted jump as large
   as the post months, and near 0 with none.
4. j and `phase` are right for January m-1 (0, pre), September m (20,
   pre), December m (23, transition), January m+1 (24, post) and
   September t1 (44, post); October of t1 is dropped.
5. A monthly row with no panel row raises; so does a panel row inside h
   with other than 46 months.
6. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5.
7. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is
   computed.
8. `results.json` carries `placebo.lead_ratio` and `estimates.finite`, and
   parses as standard JSON; the two figures are written, each under
   400 KB.
9. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
