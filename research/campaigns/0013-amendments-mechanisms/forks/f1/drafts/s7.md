# Task: Whose votes move with an allied mayor, with predetermined race controls (fork of s4, explore, idea 0010) (explore, fork f1 of campaign 0013)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0010_vote_mechanisms_controls_f1.py`, on the exploration half. It repeats step s3's analysis (`explore/0010_vote_mechanisms.py`, run `research/explore-runs/0010/45034178304.md`) with four predetermined race controls added to every fit at h. The question: do the party, sitting-deputy and state-deputy vote changes survive controls for sorting? Its `results.json` carries `placebo.t0_fail` and `estimates.finite` at the same paths as the parent, because the fork run inherits step s4's kill rules unchanged. Campaign `research/campaigns/0013-amendments-mechanisms/campaign.md`, fork of step s4 (decision 0015).

## Idea

Idea 0010 (`research/ideas/0010-amendments-share-of-quota.md`); decisions 0003, 0008, 0011 and 0015. The parent's run gave `d_party` 0.0268 (SE 0.0093) and `d_state` 0.0322 (SE 0.0095), with a clean t0 placebo. The campaign's question 3 is sorting: parties that already held the town hall win close races slightly more often (`research/explore-runs/0011/44768996266.md`). The t0 placebo checks vote levels only, not paths from t0 to t1. This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

Load the parent script `explore/0010_vote_mechanisms.py` with the `_load` pattern (`importlib.util.spec_from_file_location`, registered in `sys.modules` under a new name such as `votes0010`). Reuse its collapse, outcomes, window check, Holm, figures and `results.json` writer unchanged. Through it, also reuse the main run's functions (`prepare`, `sign_draws` with 2,000 draws and seed 20261115, `in_window`, `Sample`, `ri_summary`, `_all_finite`, `_clean`, `_paths`). Only the fitting step changes, so that it takes extra regressors.

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store, national=True)`, then `restrict` on `cd_ibge` right after each file is read.
- **Data read (referee R2).** Only `amendments_panel` (derived, v3): `panel_A`, `panel_B`, `panel_C`, found with `_paths` in `data/manifests/build/amendments_panel.json`. Nothing in `sealed/`. These are the parent's columns plus the four control sources: `party_held_mayor`, `population_m` (already read), `round` and `n_mayor_cands`.
- **The window, read from the record:** `research/explore-runs/0010/44484340952.json`, `window.h_pct`; raise unless it is 7.5. The check window is 5%, as in the parent.

### The controls (the one change)

- `party_held_mayor` (as 0 or 1), `ln(population_m)`, `round` (1 or 2, as a 0-or-1 indicator for round 2) and `n_mayor_cands`. All four are fixed before the mayoral election's result. Each has one value per (`cycle`, `cd_ibge`, `deputy_party`) group; raise if one differs within a group.
- The controls enter every fit as extra regressors, demeaned within the fixed-effect group (the `deputy` column, as in the parent). Randomisation inference uses the same race re-flips, with the controls held fixed.
- Rows with a null control, or with `population_m` null or 0, leave every fit. They are counted in `counts.dropped_null_controls`, pooled and by cycle.
- The controls apply to: the five party-level outcomes (pooled, by cycle, C minus A, and the 5% check), the deputy-level `rf_zero`, and the two t0 placebos.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

Every key of the parent's s3 draft at the same dotted path: `window.*`, `counts.*`, `party.<outcome>.<scope>`, `check_5pct.<outcome>`, `deputy.rf_zero.<scope>`, `deputy.n_not_running`, `descriptive.dose`, `placebo.party_t0`, `placebo.state_t0`, `placebo.t0_fail` (**k1**, 0 or 1, the same rule as the parent: 1 if for either placebo \|b\| > 0.10 × its mean **and** `ri_p` < 0.05), `declared.<d_party|party_minus_state>` (`p`, `p_holm`), and `estimates.finite` (**k2**). Plus `controls.names` (the four names) and `counts.dropped_null_controls`. Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite` false.

## What differs from the parent

Only the controls: `party_held_mayor`, `ln(population_m)`, `round` and `n_mayor_cands` enter as regressors in every fit at h and at the 5% check, including the placebos. Rows with a null control are dropped and counted. The rest is the parent's: outcomes, collapse, fixed effects, window, draws, declared estimates, Holm, the placebo rule, and figures (file names unchanged, inside the run's `figures/`). The descriptive dose regression also stays as in the parent, with no controls.

## Done when

1. On a synthetic panel built in the test (three cycles, at least 300 races), where `win` is correlated with `party_held_mayor` and `party_held_mayor` shifts `d_party`, the fit with controls recovers the planted jump within 10%, and the fit without them does not.
2. With controls that are pure noise, b matches the parent's fit to within 10% of its value on the same synthetic panel.
3. A control that differs within a group raises; rows with a null control are dropped and counted.
4. `t0_fail` is 1 on a synthetic panel with a planted jump in `party_share_t0` of 20% of its mean, and 0 with none.
5. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5.
6. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is computed.
7. `results.json` carries `placebo.t0_fail` and `estimates.finite` at the parent's paths and parses as standard JSON. The two figures are written, each under 400 KB.
8. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest` pass.
