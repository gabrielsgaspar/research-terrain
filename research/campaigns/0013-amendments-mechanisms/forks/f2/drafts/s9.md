# Task: When allied towns are paid, with monthly payments in ln(1 + reais per inhabitant) (explore, fork f2 of campaign 0013)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0010_payment_timing_log_f2.py`, on the exploration half. It repeats step s5's monthly payment-timing design (`research/campaigns/0013-amendments-mechanisms/drafts/s5.md`), with one change: each month's outcome is ln(1 + reais per inhabitant) instead of reais per inhabitant. Its `results.json` carries `placebo.lead_ratio` and `estimates.finite` at the same dotted paths as s5, because the fork's run inherits step s6's kill rules unchanged. Campaign `research/campaigns/0013-amendments-mechanisms/campaign.md`, fork fK of fork point s6.

## Idea

Idea 0010 (`research/ideas/0010-amendments-share-of-quota.md`). The parent run `research/explore-runs/0010/45036043356.md` found a lead ratio of 0.0257 on the level scale. The pre mean was 0.00602 and the post mean 0.235 reais per inhabitant, with large monthly SEs (0.204 at j18, 0.196 at j33). The campaign's main measure is ln(1 + reais per inhabitant). This fork asks whether the timing pattern holds on that scale. This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

Load `explore/0010_payment_timing.py` with `importlib.util.spec_from_file_location` (registered in `sys.modules` under a new name) and reuse its helpers unchanged where they take the outcome as an input: the joins, the month index j and phase, the checks, the fits, the key layout and the figures. Never edit that file. Where a parent helper builds the level outcome internally, reimplement only the outcome step here and keep everything else identical. In the same way, the parent loads `explore/0010_amendments_main.py` for `prepare`, `in_window`, `Sample`, `fe_fit`, `_col`, `_all_finite`, `_clean` and `_paths`.

## What differs from the parent

Only the outcome's functional form: every monthly, half-year and cumulative Y is built from ln(1 + that month's reais per inhabitant) instead of reais per inhabitant, for `total` and `pix`. The data, exploration half, window, months, phases, scopes, models, fixed effects, clustering, keys, figures and kill rules are as in step s5.

## Done when

1. On a synthetic panel and monthly table built in the test (three cycles, at least 300 races), a jump planted only in month j = 30 on the log scale gives b(j30) near the jump and b(j10) near 0.
2. The monthly outcome equals ln(1 + amend / population_m) on a hand-checked row, and a month with 0 payment gives 0.
3. The half-year b equals the mean of its monthly b, and the cumulative b equals the running sum (to 1e-9).
4. `lead_ratio` is above 0.5 when pre months carry a planted log-scale jump as large as the post months, and near 0 with none.
5. j and `phase` are right for j = 0, 20, 23, 24 and 44; October of t1 is dropped.
6. A monthly row with no panel row raises; so does a panel row inside h with other than 46 months; the window check raises when `window.h_pct` is not 7.5.
7. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is computed.
8. `results.json` carries `placebo.lead_ratio` and `estimates.finite` at those paths and parses as standard JSON; the two figures are written, each under 400 KB.
9. `explore/0010_payment_timing.py` is unchanged.
10. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest` pass.
