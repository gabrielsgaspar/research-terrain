# f3: The instrument and sorting checks with a local quadratic in the margin

## Why (from the ideation job)

Run 45248303745 (s2) passes k1 with a municipality-clustered F of 26.4 at h = 7.5% (b 0.185, SE 0.0359, 2300 races). Its sorting table, a side result the plan could not act on, shows the controlled jump growing with the window: 0.157 (SE 0.0631, F 6.17, 812 races) at 2.5%, 0.177 (F 16.3) at 5%, 0.185 at 7.5% and 0.209 (F 43.5) at 10%. Under the local linear model this pattern is what curvature in money against the margin would produce: the wider the window, the more of the curve the line counts as a jump. The standard check is a local quadratic at the same h. It keeps the 2300 races and changes only the polynomial in the margin, so its F can be read against the same k1, and its placebo against the same k2. The k1 pass at 7.5% is what lets the campaign go on (s2 has on_kill: stop), so a fail here would show that this pass depends on the linear form. The fork reuses amendments_panel v3, already in the bucket: one explore task and one run.

## What differs from the parent

polynomial in the margin: every linear, Poisson, controls and placebo fit adds margin^2 and win x margin^2 to win, margin and win x margin (local quadratic instead of local linear)

## What a pass or a fail would mean

Pass (survived, fork): with a quadratic in the margin, the controlled first stage still has a clustered F of at least the inherited threshold, the pre-window placebo still does not fail, and every number is finite. The report can then say that s2's first stage and its placebo do not depend on fitting a line in the margin. Fail (killed, fork): with the quadratic, F falls below the threshold, or the placebo fails, or a number is not finite. The report must then say that s2's pass rests on the local linear form, and that the growth of b with the window may be curvature, not a jump. Either way this is a further look at the same exploration half, not a finding, and it changes nothing in s3 to s6.
