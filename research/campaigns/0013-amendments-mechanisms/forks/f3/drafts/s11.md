### The one change

Add `m2` = margin^2 and `wm2` = win x margin^2 to the regressors of every fit:

- linear and controls fits: Y on win, margin, win x margin, m2 and wm2 (plus the controls where s1 has them), demeaned within deputy; the clustered variance with K = 5 + the number of controls; `f_cluster` = (b / se)^2 of win;
- randomisation: under each re-flip, `wm2` is recomputed from the flipped win, exactly as win x margin is, while `m2` and the controls stay fixed;
- by-cycle fits and cycle differences: m2 and wm2 enter interacted with the cycle dummies like margin and win x margin;
- Poisson fits: `win + margin + win:margin + m2 + wm2`; for cycle differences and Pix minus non-Pix, m2 and wm2 are also interacted with the later-cycle dummy or `is_pix`, like the other regressors;
- the placebo: the quadratic model with no controls on `amend_pre_log_pc`; `placebo.post_b` is the quadratic pooled `total` `log_pc` b with no controls; `pre_fail` = 1 if |b_pre| > 0.5 x |b_post| and the placebo's `ri_p` < 0.05, else 0.
