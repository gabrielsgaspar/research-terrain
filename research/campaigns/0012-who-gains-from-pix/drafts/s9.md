### Facts about the data

- `amendments_panel` (`data/contracts/amendments_panel.md`): one row per
  cycle, deputy and municipality where the deputy's party had a mayoral
  finalist. `margin` is that finalist's margin, and `win` = `margin` > 0.
  - `deputy` is cycle-specific (t0 `sq_candidato`).
  - `d_share` is null where `ran_t1` is false.
  - `amend_post_log_pc` = ln(1 + reais per inhabitant) in the post window
    (January m+1 to September m+2).
  - The four party-share columns are in [0, 1]. In PR #436's dry run
    they had no nulls in cycles A and B, and 2 nulls at t0 in C.
  - Exploration-half rows: A 16,533, B 12,830, C 17,067.
- `amendments_monthly` (`data/contracts/amendments_monthly.md`): exactly
  the panel's (`cycle`, `deputy`, `cd_ibge`) rows times 46 months, from
  January of m-1 to October of t1 (A 2015-01..2018-10, B 2019-01..2022-10,
  C 2023-01..2026-10). `ym` is YYYYMM. Payments are reais, nominal, ≥ 0.
  Join it to the panel on (`cycle`, `deputy`, `cd_ibge`) for `margin`,
  the race and `population_m`. Raise if a monthly row has no panel row.

### The design (fixed)

- **Model:** the 0009 `estimate`: Y on win, margin and win × margin,
  deputy (by cycle) fixed effects, |margin| ≤ 0.075, standard errors
  clustered by `cd_ibge`, race re-flips (2,000 draws, seed 20261115,
  races over every cycle together).
  - "By cycle" means the rows of one cycle; "pooled" means all three.
  - Every estimate reports b, se, the 95% interval b ± 1.96 × se, the RI
    p (except H6), the mean of Y, units and races.
  - These are secondary analyses: no alpha is claimed.
- **First stage and reduced form (figure 3).** Pooled and by cycle:
  - `first_stage`: Y = `amend_post_log_pc`;
  - `reduced_form`: Y = `d_share`, rows with `ran_t1` true.
- **H3, within-list zero-sum.** Pooled and by cycle, on rows with
  `ran_t1` true:
  - `own`: Y = `d_share`;
  - `party`: Y = `party_share_t1` − `party_share_t0`;
  - `own_minus_party`: Y = `d_share` − (`party_share_t1` −
    `party_share_t0`), one fit, so its SE is the difference's.
  - Also `party_all`: the `party` outcome on every row with non-null
    shares.
- **H4, brokerage benchmark.** Pooled and by cycle, every row with
  non-null shares: Y = `state_party_share_t1` − `state_party_share_t0`.
- **H5, selection.** Pooled and by cycle, every row: Y = `ran_t1` (0/1).
  - If the pooled 95% interval excludes 0, compute Lee bounds for H3's
    pooled `own`. Otherwise `h5.lee.computed` is false and the bounds are
    null.
  - Lee bounds, at |margin| ≤ 0.075: q_win and q_lose are the shares
    with `ran_t1` true. On the side with the higher share, keep the rows
    with `ran_t1` true and trim a fraction (q_hi − q_lo) / q_hi of them
    by `d_share`: from the top for the lower bound, from the bottom for
    the upper. Re-estimate `own` on the trimmed sample with the same
    model.
- **H6, timing.** For each month index j = 0..45 (j = 0 is January of
  m-1), on the panel rows at |margin| ≤ 0.075, two fits (`fe_fit`
  only, no randomisation inference):
  - `total`: Y = ln(1 + `amend_total` / `population_m`);
  - `pix`: Y = ln(1 + `amend_pix` / `population_m`).
  - Rows with `population_m` null or 0 leave the fit and are counted.
  - Each j has two labels: months from the inauguration, j − 24 (January
    of m+1 is j = 24), and months to the general election, j − 45
    (October of t1 is j = 45). The mayoral election (October of m) is
    j = 21.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

`<s>` is `pooled`, `A`, `B` or `C`. Every estimate block holds `b`, `se`,
`ci_low`, `ci_high`, `ri_p`, `mean`, `n_units`, `n_races`.

- `window.h_pct` (7.5) and `window.source` (the record path).
- `first_stage.<s>`, `reduced_form.<s>`.
- `h3.<s>.own`, `h3.<s>.party`, `h3.<s>.own_minus_party`,
  `h3.<s>.party_all`.
- `h4.<s>`.
- `h5.<s>`; `h5.lee.computed` (bool), `h5.lee.lower`, `h5.lee.upper`,
  `h5.lee.trim_share`.
- `h6.<total|pix>.j<NN>` (NN = 00..45, two digits) with `b`, `se`,
  `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `n_units`; `h6.months.j<NN>.ym_A` (the YYYYMM of
  that j in cycle A), `.from_inauguration`, `.to_general`;
  `h6.n_no_population`.
- `estimates.finite` (bool, **k1**): true only if every number above is
  finite (the Lee keys only when computed).

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite`
false.

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test (three cycles, 300 races, deputies
   with several municipalities) recovers planted effects within 10%:
   - a jump in `d_share` larger than the jump in the party change, with
     `own_minus_party` equal to their difference;
   - a jump in the state-deputy change.
2. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5
   (a temporary record).
3. Lee bounds:
   - with a planted effect on `ran_t1`, they are computed and bracket the
     planted `own` effect on a symmetric synthetic case;
   - with none, `h5.lee.computed` is false.
4. H6:
   - on a synthetic monthly table with a jump only in month j = 30,
     b(j30) is near the jump and b(j10) near 0;
   - the j labels are j − 24 and j − 45;
   - a monthly row with no panel row raises.
5. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is
   computed (test).
6. `results.json` carries `estimates.finite` and parses as standard JSON.
7. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
