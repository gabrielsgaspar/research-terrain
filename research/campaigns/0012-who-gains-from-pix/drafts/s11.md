# Task: seven figures for idea 0011 from the incumbency and mechanisms records, exploration half only (explore, idea 0011)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0011_figures.py`, that draws seven PNG
figures into `<out-dir>/figures/` from:

- the run records of steps s8 (`0011_incumbency_main`) and s10
  (`0011_amendments_mechanisms`);
- three exploration-half tables.

Its `results.json` carries `figures.count`, the field step s12's kill rule
reads. Campaign `research/campaigns/0012-who-gains-from-pix/campaign.md`,
step s11. Step s12 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0011 (`research/ideas/0011-who-gains-from-pix.md`). An explore run
may leave at most 8 PNGs of at most 400 KB each in `<out-dir>/figures/`
(`src/terrain/explore_figures.py`, PR #435). The record job cleans them
(`clean_png`) and links them from the run's `.md`. This is an **explore**
task (label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

## In scope

- `explore/0011_figures.py` (new).
- `tests/test_explore_figures_0011.py` (new).

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store,
  national=True)`, then `restrict` on `cd_ibge` right after each file is
  read (referee R1). No figure labels a person or shows an identifier.
- **Matplotlib only** (already a dependency), with the `Agg` backend.
- **Every figure is labelled "exploration half, not a finding"** in its
  title or as a footer line.
- **The records (read by code).** In `research/explore-runs/0011/`, for
  each of the scripts `0011_incumbency_main` and
  `0011_amendments_mechanisms`:
  - take the records `<run id>.md` whose first line starts with
    `# <script>, run ` and that have a `<run id>.json` beside them (fork
    records, `*_fN`, are never taken);
  - use the largest run id. Raise if there is none.
  - Expose this as `latest_record(directory: Path, script: str) -> dict`
    (run id and the parsed JSON).
- **Data read (referee R2).** Only these, nothing in `sealed/`, each found
  through its manifest in `data/manifests/build/` (`files`; never a
  `sealed/` path):
  - `amendments_monthly`: `monthly_A`, `monthly_B` and `monthly_C`;
    columns `cycle`, `cd_ibge`, `ym`, `amend_total`, `amend_pix`
    (payments 2015-01 to 2026-10).
  - `mayor_incumbency_panel`: `panel_2004` ... `panel_2020`; columns
    `election`, `cd_ibge`, `win`, `finalist_eligible`,
    `finalist_wins_next` (outcome years 2008-2024).
  - `amendments_panel`: `panel_A`, `panel_B` and `panel_C`; columns
    `cycle`, `cd_ibge`, `margin`, `amend_post_log_pc` (the post window,
    January m+1 to September m+2).
- **Referee R4.** This script estimates nothing. Every interval it draws
  is read from s8's or s10's record, which cluster by `cd_ibge`; binned
  means and totals carry no interval. Say so in `summary.md`.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** exactly the seven figures
  below.

### The figures (fixed; file names exact)

1. `f1_amendments_by_year.png`: individual amendment payments by calendar
   year, 2015-2026.
   - Two stacked bars per year: Pix (`amend_pix`) and other
     (`amend_total` − `amend_pix`), summed over the exploration-half rows
     of `amendments_monthly`, in millions of reais (nominal), months
     January-October only. `amendments_monthly` has no November or
     December of 2018, 2022 or 2026, so every year uses the same months;
     say so on the figure.
   - On a second axis, a line: the mayors' reelection rate at year e+4
     (2008-2024). It is the share of rows with `win` true,
     `finalist_eligible` true and `finalist_wins_next` true among rows
     with `win` and `finalist_eligible` true and a non-null
     `finalist_wins_next`.
   - Note on the figure that the payments are those of deputies in the
     panel to panel municipalities.
2. `f2_money_by_margin.png`: the mean of `amend_post_log_pc` in bins of
   1 percentage point of `margin` over [−20%, +20%].
   - One panel per cycle A, B, C, side by side.
   - A vertical line at 0 and dashed lines at ±7.5% (the window of
     record `research/explore-runs/0010/44484340952.md`).
3. `f3_first_stage_reduced_form.png`: from s10's record,
   `first_stage.<A|B|C>` and `reduced_form.<A|B|C>`, b with its 95%
   interval (`ci_low`, `ci_high`), two panels side by side.
4. `f4_monthly_event_time.png`: from s10's record, `h6.total.j<NN>` and
   `h6.pix.j<NN>`, b with 95% intervals.
   - The x axis is months from the inauguration (`from_inauguration`).
   - Vertical lines at the mayoral election (j = 21), the inauguration
     (j = 24) and the general election (j = 45), each labelled.
5. `f5_incumbency_by_cycle.png`: from s8's record,
   `h1.by_election.e<year>` for 2004-2020, b with 95% intervals.
   - x labels `2004→2008` ... `2020→2024`.
   - A horizontal line at 0.
6. `f6_h3_own_vs_party.png`: from s10's record, `h3.<A|B|C>.own` and
   `h3.<A|B|C>.party`, b with 95% intervals, side by side per cycle.
7. `f7_h2_bench_terciles.png`: from s8's record,
   `terciles.e<2016|2020>.<t1|t2|t3>`, b with 95% intervals.
   - Two series, 2016 and 2020.
   - Tercile labels from `terciles.cut1` and `terciles.cut2`.
   - A null group is drawn as a gap and noted.

Each PNG at most 400 KB (for example 1,000 × 600 pixels at dpi 100).

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

- `figures.count` (int, **k1**): the number of PNGs written.
- `figures.names` (list of the file names).
- `records.main_run_id`, `records.mechanisms_run_id`.
- `data.f1_years` (the years drawn), `data.f2_bins_per_cycle`.

`summary.md`:

- the list of figures, each with one line saying what it shows;
- the two run ids read;
- the R4 line above;
- the caveat: nothing on the exploration half is a finding.

## Out of scope

- Any estimate, interval or test computed here; any figure not listed;
  more than eight PNGs; any table not listed; the confirmation half.

## Done when

1. With two synthetic records and three small synthetic tables built in
   the test (a temporary directory and a fake store), the script writes
   the seven named PNGs, each a valid PNG under 400 KB, and
   `figures.count` is 7.
2. `latest_record`:
   - takes the largest run id;
   - ignores a fork record and a record without its `.json`;
   - raises with none.
3. Figure 1's reelection rate on a synthetic panel equals a hand count.
   Its year sums use January-October only (a test with a November
   payment).
4. Figure 2's bins cover [−20%, +20%] in 1-point steps (40 bins).
5. A tercile written null in the record does not stop the script.
6. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is
   drawn (test).
7. Every figure carries the label "exploration half, not a finding" (the
   test checks the text passed to matplotlib).
8. `results.json` carries `figures.count` and parses as standard JSON.
9. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
