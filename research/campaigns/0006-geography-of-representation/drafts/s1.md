# Task: build the section votes and polling places for all of Brazil (build, idea 0006)

## Goal

Make `tse_votes_by_section`, `tse_polling_places`, `polling_places` and
`section_votes` national, so that idea 0006 can find close council pairs and
their voters' places in every municipality. For Legal Amazon rows, the
national builds must give exactly what the current builds give. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0006-geography-of-representation/campaign.md`, step s1.
Steps s2 and s3 then acquire, and s4 and s5 build, through `data-pipeline`.

## Idea

Idea 0006 (`research/ideas/0006-geography-of-representation.md`) and
decision 0011 (`research/decisions/0011-national-scope.md`: national scope).
Data rules: `AGENTS.md` ("Data", "Personal identifiers"). These four tables
are treatment-side, so they hold every municipality and are not split
(decision 0003, option C). This is a **build** task: it may change exactly
the files below.

### Catalog

- `tse_votes_by_section`: `ufs` becomes the 26 states that elect councils,
  in this order: `[AC, AL, AM, AP, BA, CE, ES, GO, MA, MG, MS, MT, PA, PB,
  PE, PI, PR, RJ, RN, RO, RR, RS, SC, SE, SP, TO]`. **DF is left out on
  purpose**: Brasília elects no council. For every municipal year the TSE
  answers `votacao_secao_<year>_DF.zip` with HTTP 200 and a 1-byte body, so
  the zip check would fail the whole acquire (checked 2026-11-10). The URL,
  years (`[2000, 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, 2024]`) and check are
  unchanged. Rewrite `notes`: the 26 states; about 6.35 GB for the 182 zips
  (HEAD requests of 2026-11-10: 2000 0.23 GB, 2004 0.28, 2008 0.39, 2012
  0.33, 2016 1.40, 2020 1.93, 2024 1.79); the largest zip is SP 2020 at
  527 MB. The acquire re-downloads the nine Legal Amazon states into the
  new dated folder, because the generic acquirer stores every listed file
  in one folder. Keep the existing sentences about 2000, 2004 and SQ_CANDIDATO.
- `tse_polling_places`: no change to `acquire`. The TSE publishes one
  national zip per year (`eleitorado_local_votacao_<year>.zip`, 32-46 MB),
  and the raw folder `data/raw/tse/polling_places/` already holds all of
  Brazil. Add one sentence to `notes`: "National: one file per year for
  all states; the acquire of campaign 0006 step s3 is expected to report
  unchanged."
- `polling_places`, `section_votes`: `notes` say "Brazil" instead of "the
  Legal Amazon"; `polling_places` notes also mention `nr_cep`.

### polling_places: national

- Keep every municipality with a `cd_tse` in the `municipalities` table,
  not only `legal_amazon`. `check_legal_amazon` becomes a check that every
  `cd_ibge` is in the `municipalities` table (rename it; same error style).
- New output column `nr_cep` (text, 8 digits with leading zeros, or null):
  - from the TSE file's `NR_CEP` for 2012 onward (add `nr_cep` to
    `TSE_COLUMNS`; the phone column `NR_TELEFONE_LOCAL` stays unread);
  - from Hidalgo's `nr_cep` for 2008, and wherever the TSE row has none.
    Hidalgo's `geocoded_polling_stations.csv.gz` has the column `nr_cep`
    (see `data/fixtures/hidalgo/geocoded_polling_stations_sample.csv`).
  - Strip it and left-pad it to 8 digits. A value that is not 1-8 digits
    after stripping becomes null; keep no `_raw` column. Count nulls per
    year in the notes (`cep_missing_share`).
  - A place whose TSE rows carry several distinct CEPs takes the most
    frequent one, with ties to the smallest. Count those places
    (`two_cep_places`).
- Everything else is unchanged: the 5% missing-coordinate cap and the 0.1%
  unparsed cap per year, the coordinate rules, and `panel_id`.
- The manifest notes add a count of places per state per year. Values
  only, no names or addresses.

### section_votes: national

- Keep every municipality with a `cd_tse`. `process_file`'s `legal`
  argument becomes the national table; rename it `municipalities`. The
  `rows_outside_legal_amazon` note becomes `rows_without_municipality`. It
  counts rows whose `sg_ue` is not in the table, and it should be 0.
- **Memory.** SP 2020 is a 527 MB zip, about 6-8 GB of Latin-1 CSV.
  `read_section_zip` today decodes the whole member into one string,
  re-encodes it and reads every column, which needs several times that in
  RAM. The runner has 16 GB. Change it to stream:
  - copy the member to a temporary UTF-8 file in chunks of at most 64 MB,
    decoding Latin-1 as it goes;
  - read that file with `pl.scan_csv(..., separator=";",
    infer_schema=False)`, selecting only the needed columns before
    `collect()`;
  - delete the temporary file and the zip as soon as the state-year is
    read.
  
  Keep every check `read_section_zip` makes today (missing column, empty
  key, non-integer value) and the same column types.
- **Coverage.** `MIN_COVERAGE_SHARE` (0.8) stays as the floor for 2008-2024.
  For 2004, coverage is reported per state and never raises. 2004 is not
  used by idea 0006, and its national coverage is unknown (the Legal Amazon
  had 87%). Say this in the constant's comment and the contract.
- `MIN_AGREE_SHARE` and `MAX_UNMATCHED_SHARE` are unchanged.
- The notes keep `municipalities_with_sections=covered/total (by state:
  ...)` for all 26 states.
