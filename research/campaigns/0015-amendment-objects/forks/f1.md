# f1: What allied towns get, with the no-plan Pix line fitted in log_pc only (no Poisson fit for no_plan)

## Why (from the ideation job)

Run 45061701322 was killed by k2 alone. The only non-finite value is pix_groups.no_plan.poisson.C: n_obs 0, with b, se and p written as null. The other no_plan Poisson fits are degenerate: B has b 19.8 (se 2.2) on 119 rows, and pooled B+C has b 146 (se 29.7), a premium of 2.75e+65%. A Poisson fit cannot identify a premium on an outcome that is zero in nearly every deputy group. Everything else is finite. k1 is not met, because visible has a pooled B+C F of 41.5 (services 1.75, fungible 3.72). The declared estimates are pix_minus_tied b 1.04 (se 0.427, Holm p 0.0458), visible_minus_fungible b -0.805 (se 1.13) and health_c_minus_a b 1.29 (se 0.632). The no_plan line is descriptive in the s4 draft. no_plan money is already inside fungible, and the line's log_pc fits stay finite (pooled b 0.0117, se 0.0076). Dropping only the no_plan Poisson fits shows whether the design is killed by anything other than that one empty, descriptive cell. The fork changes nothing in any declared estimate, group, instrument, window or kill rule. It reuses amendments_panel and amendments_objects, already in the bucket: one explore task and one run.

## What differs from the parent

models fitted: the descriptive pix_groups.no_plan line is estimated by the linear log_pc fit only; its three Poisson fits (B, C, pooled_bc) are not run

## What a pass or a fail would mean

Pass (survived, fork): with no Poisson fit for the no-plan line, at least one Pix group still has a strong first stage, and every remaining estimate, standard error and p-value is finite. The step's numbers (instrument premiums, group premiums and the three declared estimates) then stand as a design the close-out can weigh for the confirmation half, next to the count of forks tried (1). The no-plan line is reported in log_pc only. Fail (killed, fork): some other estimate is not finite, or no group is strong. The design then has a defect beyond the empty no-plan cell, and the close-out should not recommend it as it stands. Either way it is a further look at the same exploration half, not a finding.
