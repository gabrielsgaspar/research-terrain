# Task: the gate for what allied towns get: measure quality, plan coverage, the Pix first stage and its placebo (explore, idea 0013)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0013_objects_gate.py`, on the exploration
half. Before any analysis by instrument or plan class, it checks four
things:

- the Pix work-plan measure is reliable (kappa and cross-validated macro-F1
  on groups);
- most Pix money has a plan;
- Pix money jumps where the deputy's party narrowly won (cycles B and C);
- in cycle C there is no comparable jump before the mayoral election.

Its `results.json` carries `measure.kappa_groups`,
`measure.cv_macro_f1_groups`, `measure.pix_value_with_plan`,
`first_stage.f_cluster`, `placebo.pre_fail` and `estimates.finite`, the
fields step s3's kill rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0015-amendment-objects/campaign.md`, step s2. Step s3
runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0013 (`research/ideas/0013-what-allies-get.md`); decisions 0003,
0008, 0011 and 0018; campaign 0011's gate record
`research/explore-runs/0010/44484340952.md` (the window) and main run
`research/explore-runs/0010/44538371996.md`. This is an **explore** task
(label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

The linear estimator is the one of `explore/0010_amendments_main.py`.
- **Loading it:** use the `_load` pattern that `explore/0010_payment_timing.py`
  uses (`importlib.util.spec_from_file_location`, registered in
  `sys.modules` as `main0010`).
- **Reuse, unchanged:** from it, or through its `gate` and `gate.g9`
  modules where it does not re-export a name:
  - `prepare` (adds `race`, 0..R-1 over sorted (`cycle`, `cd_ibge`));
  - `sign_draws` (2,000 draws, seed 20261115), `n_races`, `in_window`;
  - `estimate` and `full_fit` (b, se, `ri_p`, `mean`, `n_units`,
    `n_races`, `f_cluster` = (b / se)²): the model with deputy fixed
    effects, errors clustered by `cd_ibge`, and the randomisation p from
    race re-flips;
  - `GateError`, `_all_finite`, `_clean`, `_paths`.
- **Deputies:** `deputy` is the t0 `sq_candidato`, so it is specific to a
  cycle, and deputy fixed effects are deputy-by-cycle fixed effects.

### Facts about the data

- `data/contracts/amendments_panel.md` and `data/contracts/amendments_objects.md`.
- **Rows.** One row per cycle, deputy and municipality where the deputy's
  party had a mayoral finalist. `margin` is that finalist's margin, and
  `win` = `margin` > 0. `amendments_objects` has exactly the panel's rows.
  Join on (`cycle`, `deputy`, `cd_ibge`), and raise `GateError` if a
  panel row of B or C has no objects row or the reverse.
- **Pix columns.** `obj_<w>_pix` is the deputy's Pix payments to the town
  in window `<w>`, in reais, floored at 0. It must equal the panel's
  `amend_<w>_pix`. Count the rows where they differ by more than R$1
  (`checks.pix_mismatch`) and raise `GateError` if there is any.
- **Pix timing.** Pix exists from 2020, so it is used in cycles B and C
  only. Cycle A is not read.
- **Exploration-half rows** (manifest of 2026-11-20): B 12,830 and C
  17,067. At 7.5% the gate record has 2,300 races and 13,809 rows over
  the three cycles.
- **`measurement.parquet`**, long format: `measure` (text), `cycle` (text
  or null), `year` (int or null), `class` (text or null), `value` (float).
  - `kappa_groups` and `cv_macro_f1_groups`: one row each, with `cycle`,
    `year` and `class` null.
  - `pix_value_with_plan`: one row per cycle with Pix (`cycle` "B" and
    "C"): the share of exploration-half post-window Pix reais, on the
    panel's rows, with a matched work plan.
  - Other measures in the file are not read here.
  - Raise `GateError` if a needed row is missing or appears twice.

### What to compute

**(a) The measure** (copied from `measurement.parquet`, no computation):

- `measure.kappa_groups`, `measure.cv_macro_f1_groups`;
- `measure.pix_value_with_plan_by_cycle.<B|C>`;
- `measure.pix_value_with_plan` = the smaller of B and C (**k3**).

**(b) The Pix first stage.**
- **Outcome:** `pix_post_log_pc` = ln(1 + `obj_post_pix` / `population_m`).
  Rows with `population_m` null or 0 leave every fit and are counted
  (`counts.no_population`).
- **Fit:** `full_fit` at h on cycles B and C pooled (`prepare` on the
  B+C table, draws from `sign_draws` over its races).
- **Report:** b, se, the 95% interval b ± 1.96 × se, `ri_p`, `mean`,
  `n_units`, `n_races`, `f_cluster`; also at 5% (`check_5pct`).
- `first_stage.f_cluster` is the h fit's (**k4**).

**(c) The placebo, cycle C only.**
- **Fits:** `full_fit` at h on cycle C's rows (`prepare` on the C table,
  draws over its races) for `pix_pre_log_pc` = ln(1 + `obj_pre_pix` /
  `population_m`) and for `pix_post_log_pc`.
- **Rule:** `placebo.pre_fail` = 1 if |b_pre| > 0.5 × |b_post| **and**
  the pre fit's `ri_p` < 0.05, else 0 (an integer) (**k5**).

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

- `window.h_pct` (7.5), `window.check_pct` (5), `window.source` (the
  record path).
- `counts.units_h`, `counts.races_h` (B+C at h),
  `counts.by_cycle.<B|C>.units`, `.races`, `counts.no_population`.
- `checks.pix_mismatch` (0 on a passing run).
- `measure.kappa_groups`, `measure.cv_macro_f1_groups`,
  `measure.pix_value_with_plan`, `measure.pix_value_with_plan_by_cycle.<B|C>`.
- `first_stage`: `b`, `se`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `ri_p`, `mean`,
  `n_units`, `n_races`, `f_cluster` (**k4**).
- `check_5pct.first_stage`: `b`, `se`, `ri_p`, `f_cluster`.
- `placebo.pre`, `placebo.post`: each `b`, `se`, `ri_p`, `mean`,
  `n_units`. Also `placebo.ratio` (|b_pre| / |b_post|) and
  `placebo.pre_fail` (**k5**, 0 or 1).
- `estimates.finite` (**k6**, bool): true only if every number above is
  finite.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite`
false. `results.json` must stay under 200 KB (`write_results` enforces
it).

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel and objects table built in the test (cycles B and C,
   at least 300 races, deputies with several municipalities) recover a
   planted Pix jump in `pix_post_log_pc` within 10%.
2. `pre_fail` is 1 with a planted cycle C pre-window jump larger than
   half the post jump, and 0 with none.
3. The measure fields are read from a synthetic `measurement` table:
   `pix_value_with_plan` is the smaller of B and C. A missing or
   duplicated measure row raises.
4. A Pix mismatch of more than R$1 between `obj_post_pix` and
   `amend_post_pix`, or a panel row without an objects row, raises.
5. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5 (a
   temporary record).
6. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before anything is
   computed.
7. `results.json` carries every key the kill rules read
   (`measure.kappa_groups`, `measure.cv_macro_f1_groups`,
   `measure.pix_value_with_plan`, `first_stage.f_cluster`,
   `placebo.pre_fail`, `estimates.finite`) and parses as standard JSON.
   The figure is written, under 400 KB.
8. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
