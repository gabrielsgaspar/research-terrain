# Task: what allied towns get, by instrument and by the declared use of Pix, at campaign 0011's 7.5% window (explore, idea 0013)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0013_objects_main.py`, on the exploration
half. It splits the amendment premium of towns where the deputy's party
narrowly won the mayoralty by instrument (Pix, health, tied) and, within
Pix, by the declared use of the work plans (visible, services, fungible).
It also reports the three estimates idea 0013 declared. Its
`results.json` carries `first_stage.n_strong` and `estimates.finite`, the
fields step s5's kill rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0015-amendment-objects/campaign.md`, step s4. Step s5
runs it through `explore-run`.

### Facts about the data

`data/contracts/amendments_objects.md`. Rows are exactly the panel's.
Join on (`cycle`, `deputy`, `cd_ibge`), and raise `GateError` if a row is
missing on either side.

- **Instrument columns** (reais, nominal, each floored at 0):

- **Pix by group:** `obj_post_pix_visible`, `obj_post_pix_services`,

- **Exploration-half rows** (v3 panel): A 16,533, B 12,830, C 17,067.
- **`measurement.parquet`**, long format (`measure`, `cycle`, `year`,

    group or "0"/"1"/"2");
  - `legacy_share_rows` (by `year`, or null);
  - all of these over exploration-half plans.

### Outcomes and models (fixed)

For each outcome `<y>` (reais):

- `log_pc`: ln(1 + reais / `population_m`);
- `poisson`: reais, by Poisson.

Rows with `population_m` null or 0 leave every fit and are counted
(`counts.no_population`).

- **Linear:** `full_fit` at h (pooled scopes, with draws over the scope's
  races), and `by_cycle_fit` for A, B, C and C minus A. For Pix by cycle,
  `full_fit` on each cycle's rows. Report b, se, the 95% interval
  b ± 1.96 × se and, for `full_fit`, `ri_p`, `mean`, `n_units`,
  `n_races`, `f_cluster`.
- **Poisson:** `pyfixest.fepois` of reais on `win + margin + win:margin`.
  - **Model:** deputy fixed effects, `offset` ln(`population_m`),
    `vcov={"CRV1": "cd_ibge"}`, rows at |margin| ≤ h.
  - **Report:** b, se, the two-sided p (pyfixest's), the premium
    100 × (exp(b) − 1) and its 95% interval
    100 × (exp(b ± 1.96 × se) − 1), and the rows used (`n_obs`).
  - **By cycle:** a separate fit per cycle.
  - **C minus A:** one fit on A and C rows, with every regressor also
    interacted with a dummy `isC`. The difference is the coefficient of
    `win:isC`; its point estimate equals the difference of the separate
    fits (test).
  - **One outcome minus another** (B and C pooled): stack the rows
    twice, with a dummy for the first outcome. Fixed effects are deputy ×
    outcome, and every regressor is also interacted with the dummy. The
    difference is the coefficient of `win:<dummy>`; its point estimate
    equals the difference of the two pooled fits (test).
  - **Dropped groups:** fixed-effect groups pyfixest drops (all-zero
    outcome) are dropped, and the rows used are reported.

### What to estimate

**(a) Instruments.** `pix` (cycles B and C), `health` and `tied` (A, B
and C), each `log_pc` and `poisson`:

- pooled: B+C for Pix, A+B+C for health and tied;
- by cycle;
- `c_minus_a`, for health and tied;
- at 5%: the pooled estimate only.

**(b) Pix groups.** `visible`, `services`, `fungible`, each `log_pc` and
`poisson`:

- B+C pooled (`pooled_bc`), B and C;
- `no_plan` the same way, as a descriptive line.

`first_stage.n_strong` = how many of the three groups have a pooled B+C
`log_pc` `f_cluster` of at least 10 (an integer, 0 to 3).

**(c) Composition (descriptive, no estimate).**
- **Copied from `measurement.parquet`:** class, group and specificity
  shares of exploration-half plan value by amendment year and for all
  years, and the Legado row share by year and for all years.
- **Computed:** within h, the share of each group in the post-window Pix
  reais of rows with `win` true and of rows with `win` false, by cycle (B,
  C). Plain sums, no model.

**Declared estimates** (three; p-values adjusted together by Holm's
method):

- `pix_minus_tied`: the stacked Poisson Pix minus tied, B+C pooled;
- `visible_minus_fungible`: the stacked Poisson visible minus fungible
  (both Pix), B+C pooled;
- `health_c_minus_a`: the interacted Poisson health C minus A.

Everything else is descriptive, and `summary.md` says so.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

- `window.h_pct`, `window.check_pct`, `window.source`.
- `counts.units_h`, `counts.races_h`, `counts.by_cycle.<A|B|C>.units`,
  `.races`, `counts.no_population`, `counts.sum_gap`.
- `instruments.<pix|health|tied>.<log_pc|poisson>.<scope>`:
  - `<scope>` is `pooled`, `A`, `B`, `C` or `c_minus_a`, as listed above;
  - linear `full_fit` scopes: `b`, `se`, `ci_low`, `ci_high`, `ri_p`,
    `mean`, `n_units`, `n_races`, `f_cluster`;
  - linear by-cycle and difference scopes: `b`, `se`, `ci_low`,
    `ci_high`;
  - Poisson: `b`, `se`, `p`, `premium_pct`, `premium_ci_low`,
    `premium_ci_high`, `n_obs`.
- `check_5pct.<inst>.<measure>`: `b`, `se`, and `ri_p` or `p`.
- `pix_groups.<visible|services|fungible|no_plan>.<log_pc|poisson>.<pooled_bc|B|C>`:
  the same fields as above.
- `composition.measurement.<measure>.<all|YYYY>.<class>`: the copied
  shares. `composition.legacy_share_rows.<all|YYYY>`.
- `composition.within_h.<B|C>.<win|lose>.<visible|services|fungible>`:
  shares.
- `differences.<pix_minus_tied|visible_minus_fungible|health_c_minus_a>`:
  `b`, `se`, `p`, `ratio_pct` (100 × (exp(b) − 1)), `n_obs`.
- `declared.<name>`: `p`, `p_holm`.
- `first_stage.n_strong` (**k1**, integer 0 to 3),
  `first_stage.f_cluster.<visible|services|fungible>`.
- `estimates.finite` (**k2**, bool): true only if every estimate above is
  finite.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and make `estimates.finite`
false. A composition share with no money under it (0 / 0) is written as
`null` and does not count against `estimates.finite`. `results.json`
must stay under 200 KB.

   at least 300 races, deputies with several municipalities, Pix only in

   and a planted Poisson premium of 30% within 5 percentage points.
2. The Poisson health C minus A, Pix minus tied and visible minus
   fungible point estimates equal the differences of the separate fits.
3. `first_stage.n_strong` is 3 on a synthetic table with strong jumps in
   all three groups, and 0 with none.
4. Holm: for p-values (0.01, 0.04, 0.03) the adjusted values are
   (0.03, 0.06, 0.06).
5. The window check raises when the record's `window.h_pct` is not 7.5.
   A panel row without an objects row raises.

   computed.
7. `results.json` carries `first_stage.n_strong` and `estimates.finite`
   and parses as standard JSON. The three figures are written, each under
   400 KB.

   pass.
