# Task: build the mining panel by municipality and year, sealed by half (build, idea 0007)

## Goal

A new build, `mining_panel`: MapBiomas mining area per Brazilian
municipality and year, 1985-2025, plus each municipality's 2004
native-vegetation share as a baseline control. It is outcome data, split by
half: exploration-half rows go to derived, every other row only to sealed,
like `cell_outcomes` and `place_outcomes`. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0007-gold-and-the-ballot/campaign.md`, step s12. Step
s13 runs it. Step s14's first-stage script reads its derived half.

## Idea

Idea 0007 (`research/ideas/0007-gold-and-the-ballot.md`); decisions 0003 and
0011. The catalog name, `derived: data/derived/mining_panel/` and `sealed:
sealed/outcomes/mining_panel/` are fixed by the campaign 0007 PR. The sealed
line is pinned in `tests/test_split.py`. Do not change those three lines;
add the contract and fill in the rest of the entry. The model is
`src/terrain/builds/place_outcomes.py`, which reads the national
`mapbiomas_coverage_national` rasters, polygons and both exploration lists
(`read_lists`, `read_raster`, `flat_band`) and writes derived and sealed
halves. This is a **build** task: it may change exactly the files below.

### Inputs (from `data/manifests/`)

- `mapbiomas_mining` (raw, step s11): `mining_shares_<year>.tif`, 1985-2025.
  Four `uint16` bands in catalog order (`garimpo_gold`, `garimpo_other`,
  `industrial_gold`, `industrial_other`), each the cell's share × 10,000, on
  the national grid of the catalog entry.
- `mapbiomas_coverage_national` (raw): `coverage_shares_2004.tif`, band
  `native` only, the same grid.
- `ibge_boundaries_2024` (raw): `BR_Municipios_2024`, dropping 4300001 and
  4300002.
- The two exploration lists, through `place_outcomes.read_lists` or the same
  logic. A code in both lists stops the build.

### Cells to municipalities

- A cell belongs to the municipality whose polygon contains the cell's
  centre (Albers, `terrain.grid`).
- Use a bounding-box prefilter per polygon and `shapely.contains_xy`.
- A municipality with no cell centre (very small) gets the one cell that
  contains its polygon's representative point. List these in the manifest
  notes.
- Cells whose centre lies in no polygon (ocean, outside Brazil) are dropped.

### `mining_panel_<year>.parquet`: one row per municipality (5,571) per year

- `cd_ibge`, `year`, `n_cells`;
- `garimpo_gold_ha`, `garimpo_other_ha`, `industrial_gold_ha`,
  `industrial_other_ha`: the sum over the municipality's cells of
  share / 10,000 × 100 ha;
- `garimpo_ha = garimpo_gold_ha + garimpo_other_ha`;
- `garimpo_share = garimpo_ha / (n_cells × 100)`;
- `native_share_2004`: the mean of the 2004 native share over the cells,
  0 to 1.

Exploration-half rows (either list) go to derived; all other rows go only
to the sealed folder. The build creates objects in sealed and never reads
them.

### Checks that stop the build

- A raster's shape, dtype or band count differs from the catalog.
- Any year 1985-2025 is missing.
- In any cell, the four bands sum to more than 10,000.
- A municipality is in the boundaries but not in either half's list, or
  the reverse.
- National `garimpo_ha` in 2022 is below 100,000 ha or above 1,000,000 ha.
  Cortinhas Ferreira Neto et al. (2024) report about 263,000 ha (2,627 km²)
  of garimpo in 2022 from MapBiomas: a value far off means a units bug.

### Output path and manifest

- Output: `<derived>/<hash>/` and `<sealed>/<hash>/`. The hash covers this
  module's code, the `terrain` modules it imports, and the sorted input
  SHA-256s.
- Upload only after every check passes.
- Manifest notes count **exploration-half rows only**: municipalities, and
  `garimpo_ha` for 1985, 2000, 2012 and 2024. Never a confirmation-half
  number.

## Out of scope

- Any regression; reading the sealed folder; changing the raw data or
  `tests/test_split.py`.
