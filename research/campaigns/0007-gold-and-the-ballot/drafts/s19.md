# Task: acquire mining-sector campaign receipts and build the politics panel, sealed by half (build, idea 0007)

## Goal

Two things:

- the `tse_receipts` acquire: Base dos Dados candidate receipts for
  2004-2012, summed in BigQuery by year, municipality, office and donor
  CNAE. No donor CPF, CNPJ or name is ever selected;
- a new build, `politics_panel`: one row per municipality and municipal
  election 2000-2024, with the nine outcomes of idea 0007. It is split by
  half like `cell_outcomes`.

Campaign `research/campaigns/0007-gold-and-the-ballot/campaign.md`, step
s19. Step s20 runs the acquire and step s21 the build. Step s22's script
reads the derived half.

### Catalog entry `tse_receipts`

- `source`: "TSE candidate campaign receipts, via Base dos Dados:
  basedosdados.br_tse_eleicoes.receitas_candidato; summed by year,
  municipality, office and donor CNAE".
- `licence`: "TSE open data: CC BY (dadosabertos.tse.jus.br); Base dos Dados
  lists no licence".
- `gcs: data/raw/tse/receipts/`; `status: planned`.
- `bigquery:`, with endpoint, scope, location and bytes as `inep_schools`
  and no `page`:
  - `table: basedosdados.br_tse_eleicoes.receitas_candidato`;
  - all 27 states; `years: [2004, 2012]`;
  - `aggregate: {group_by: [ano, sigla_uf, id_municipio, cargo, cnae_2_doador], count: n_receitas, sums: [valor_receita]}`;
  - `min_rows: 10000`.
- `notes`:
  - The columns come from Base dos Dados' metadata (2026-11-11). It has 55
    columns, including `ano`, `sigla_uf`, `id_municipio` (IBGE),
    `cargo`, `cnae_2_doador` and `valor_receita`; it is partitioned by
    `ano`.
  - Donor CPF/CNPJ and names are never selected; `terrain.bigquery`
    refuses them.
  - Which years have `cnae_2_doador` filled is unconfirmed. TSE's own files
    carry a donor sector only in 2012, as text.
  - Company donations were banned from 2015, so the series stops at 2012.
    The general elections of 2006 and 2010 fall in the year range, and the
    build keeps only municipal offices.
  - Fallback: TSE's zips (`prestacao_contas_2004.zip`, `_2008.zip`,
    `prestacao_final_2012.zip`). They carry no CNAE.

- `tse_results` (derived): `candidate_votes_<year>.parquet` and

- `municipalities` (derived): `cd_ibge`, `cd_tse`, `uf`.
- `sim_homicides` (raw, step s17): `ano`, `id_municipio_ocorrencia`,
  `n_obitos`.
- `ibge_population_annual` (raw, step s18): `ano`, `id_municipio`,

- `tse_receipts` (raw, step s20): `ano`, `id_municipio`, `cargo`,
  `cnae_2_doador`, `n_receitas`, `sum_valor_receita`.
- The two exploration lists.

### Columns: one row per `cd_ibge` × `election` (2000, 2004, ..., 2024)

Council is `cd_cargo` 13, mayor `cd_cargo` 11. Use the main election
(`main_election`), first round. A candidate "ran" if their first-round
votes are above 0 in `tse_results`. This avoids reading the
candidacy-status fields, which change across years.

1. `cands_per_seat` = council candidates who ran / council seats filled
   (candidates with elected status, `elected_status`).
2. `mining_cand_share`: share of council candidates who ran whose
   `cd_ocupacao` is 605 (GARIMPEIRO) or 253 (TRABALHADOR DE MINAS E
   PEDREIRAS...). Also `n_mining_cands`.
3. `enc_mayor` = 1 / Σ s_i², with s_i each mayoral candidate's share of the
   candidates' first-round votes.
4. `margin_mayor` = (first minus second) / valid votes, first round. It is
   1.0 when there was one candidate.
5. `seat_turnover` = 1 − Σ_p min(seats_p,e, seats_p,e−4) / seats_e, over
   parties p (`sg_partido` of the elected councillors). Null for 2000.
6. `mining_councillor_share`: share of elected councillors with code 605
   or 253.
7. `incumbent_reelected`: share of the councillors elected at e−4 who are
   elected at e. Link them by `nr_cpf_candidato`, **in memory only**. A CPF
   that is empty or not 11 digits is unlinked; count those per year in the
   manifest notes. Null for 2000. No CPF, name or hash is written anywhere.
8. Mining money, for elections 2004, 2008 and 2012 only:
   - Receipts of offices "vereador" or "prefeito", compared lower-cased
     and accent-stripped. Stop the build if neither value appears in a
     year.
   - `mining_money_share` = sum of `sum_valor_receita` with a mining CNAE
     / sum of all.
   - A mining CNAE: take `cnae_2_doador` digits only, left-padded to 7.
     Then divisions 07 or 08, or the first four digits 0990.
   - Also `receipts_total`, `receipts_with_cnae` (the value with a
     non-null CNAE) and `n_receipts_with_cnae`. Null for other elections.
9. `homicide_rate` = Σ homicides over years e−4..e−1 / Σ population over
   the same years × 100,000. Municipality of occurrence. Also
   `homicides_sum`. Null if any of the four population years is missing.

Also `uf`, `n_council_cands`, `n_seats`, `valid_votes_mayor` and
`n_mayor_cands`. A municipality that did not exist at an election (no
results row) has no row for it.

Exploration-half rows go to derived, all others only to sealed, as one
Parquet per election.

### Checks that stop the build

- A `cd_tse` in the results with no `cd_ibge`.
- `cands_per_seat` below 1 in any row.
- Shares outside [0, 1].
- Fewer than 5,000 municipalities in any election from 2004 on.
- `homicide_rate` above 1,000 per 100,000.
- National mining-occupation candidates summed over 2004-2012 below 100.
  The full files have 605 alone at 122, 108 and 65 in 2004, 2008 and 2012.

### Output path and manifest

- Output: `<derived>/<hash>/` and `<sealed>/<hash>/`. The hash covers this
  module, the `terrain` modules it imports, and the sorted input SHA-256s.
- Upload after every check.
- Notes count **exploration-half rows only**:
  - rows per election;
  - unlinked CPFs per election (counts only);
  - years with any receipt CNAE.

   pass.
