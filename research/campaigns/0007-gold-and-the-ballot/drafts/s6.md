# Task: build gold exposure per municipality and the real gold price series (build, idea 0007)

## Goal

A new build, `gold_exposure`. It writes two files: one row per Brazilian
municipality with its gold exposure, fixed by geology and by pre-2000 ANM
titles, and one row per year with the real gold and soybean prices. Both
are treatment-side data (decision 0003), so they are not split by half.
Campaign `research/campaigns/0007-gold-and-the-ballot/campaign.md`, step s6.
Step s7 runs it. Steps s8, s14 and s22 read it.

### `gold_exposure.parquet`: one row per municipality (5,571)

All areas are in the Albers projection of `terrain.grid`, in km².

- `cd_ibge`, `uf`, `legal_amazon`;
- `area_km2`;
- `centroid_x_km`, `centroid_y_km`: the polygon centroid in Albers km, for
  Conley standard errors.
- **Geology (main exposure):**
  - Each SGB point is assigned to the municipality polygon that contains
    it. Points with missing or non-numeric coordinates are counted in the
    notes, and so are points outside every polygon. Both are dropped.
  - A **gold occurrence** is a point whose `substancias` field, split on
    commas and each item stripped, has an item equal to "Ouro". Compare
    case-insensitively and accent-insensitively (NFKD, marks removed).
    "Ouro" inside another word does not count.
  - Columns:
    - `geo_n_gold`: number of gold occurrences;
    - `geo_gold_per_km2 = geo_n_gold / area_km2`;
    - `geo_exposed = geo_n_gold >= 1`;
    - `geo_n_gold_garimpo`: gold occurrences with `status_economico` equal
      to "Garimpo" (same comparison);
    - `geo_n_gold_indigenous`: gold occurrences inside an Indigenous land
      (CNFP 2025);
    - `geo_gold_indigenous_share`: that count over `geo_n_gold`, or null
      when `geo_n_gold` is 0.
- **ANM titles (robustness exposure; active titles only):**
  - Gold titles have `subs` in {"OURO", "MINÉRIO DE OURO", "OURO
    NATIVO"}, exact match after stripping.
  - Garimpo titles also have `fase` in {"LAVRA GARIMPEIRA", "REQUERIMENTO
    DE LAVRA GARIMPEIRA"}.
  - Sand-and-gravel (placebo) titles have `subs` in {"AREIA", "AREIA
    COMUM", "AREIA LAVADA", "CASCALHO", "SAIBRO"}.
  - Pre-2000 means `ano` < 2000. A valid year is an integer from 1900 to
    2026.
  - Repair invalid polygons with `shapely.make_valid`, and count the
    repairs in the notes.
  - A title counts in every municipality its polygon intersects.
  - Columns:
    - `title_n_gold`: all active gold titles;
    - `title_n_gold_dated`: those with a valid `ano`;
    - `title_n_gold_pre2000`, `title_n_garimpo_pre2000`;
    - `title_gold_pre2000_share`: area of the union of the pre-2000 gold
      title polygons, intersected with the municipality, over `area_km2`;
    - `title_garimpo_pre2000_share`: the same for pre-2000 garimpo titles;
    - `title_sand_pre2000_share`: the same for pre-2000 sand and gravel
      titles;
    - `title_exposed = title_n_gold_pre2000 >= 1`.
- **Control:** `indigenous_share`, the share of the municipality's area
  inside Indigenous lands (CNFP 2025, union of polygons).

### `gold_prices.parquet`: one row per calendar year with 12 months of all series

- The xlsx: resolve sheet "Monthly Prices" through `xl/workbook.xml` and
  `xl/_rels/workbook.xml.rels`; do not assume `sheet2.xml`.
- Find the columns by their header text in row 5: "Gold" and "Soybeans".
- Months come from column A (`YYYYMmm`). "…" or empty cells are missing.
- CPI: `CPIAUCSL.csv`, monthly. `observation_date` is `YYYY-MM-01`.
- Columns:
  - `year`;
  - `gold_usd` and `soy_usd`: annual means of the monthly nominal prices;
  - `cpi`: annual mean;
  - `gold_real` and `soy_real`: in 2024 dollars, `price × cpi_2024 / cpi`;
  - `ln_gold_real`, `ln_soy_real`;
  - `n_months_gold`, `n_months_soy`, `n_months_cpi`.
- Keep only years in which all three series have 12 months (1960 onward;
  2026 is dropped as incomplete).

### Checks that stop the build

- Fewer than 5,571 municipalities with a polygon. Any `cd_ibge` in
  `municipalities` without one.
- SGB rows below 30,000, or gold occurrences below 4,000 (36,484 and 4,478
  on 2026-11-11).
- ANM rows below 250,000. Gold titles with `ano` < 2000 below 9,000 (10,810
  on 2026-11-11).
- Gold price years below 60. Any year from 1985 to 2025 missing.
- Any share outside [0, 1] beyond a 1e-9 tolerance.
