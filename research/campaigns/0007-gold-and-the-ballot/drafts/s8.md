# Task: feasibility counts for the gold-and-the-ballot design, no outcome data (explore, idea 0007)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0007_gold_feasibility.py`. It counts whether
idea 0007's exposure can carry a municipality panel: enough gold
municipalities, spread over enough states, title years usable, plus three
informational counts. Its `results.json` carries the four fields that step
s9's kill rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0007-gold-and-the-ballot/campaign.md`, step s8. Step s9
runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0007 (`research/ideas/0007-gold-and-the-ballot.md`); decision 0011
(national scope). This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may
change only the two files below.

## In scope

- `explore/0007_gold_feasibility.py` (new).
- `tests/test_explore_gold_feasibility.py` (new).

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national.** Call
  `exploration_municipalities(store, national=True)` and `restrict` every
  table on `cd_ibge` before computing anything. `tests/test_explore.py`
  checks both; idea 0007 is in `terrain.explore.NATIONAL_IDEAS`.
- **No outcome data (referee R2).**
  - It reads only:
    - `gold_exposure` (both files);
    - `tse_results` (`candidate_votes_<year>.parquet` and
      `turnout_<year>.parquet`, for the close-pair count);
    - `tse_candidates` (occupation codes, for one pooled count).
  - It reads no MapBiomas data, no SIM data, no `mining_panel` and no
    `politics_panel`.
  - The occupation count is pooled over the whole exploration half by
    election. It is **never split by exposure**, so it shows no effect.
  - Margins of close races are the treatment side of the close-race design
    (decisions 0004, 0008), not an outcome.
- **No inference.** The script makes no estimate. Referee R4 ("level and
  inference") has nothing to check here: every count is at the
  municipality level, the unit of the design, and nothing is estimated.
  If the referee marks R4 "Not applicable", the gate stops on it; that is a
  known issue (campaign 0003, PR #264). It is recorded in the report, not
  fixed by a design change.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** no regression, no comparison of
  exposed with unexposed places, no verdict on the kill rules.
- **Data access:** read paths from the manifests in `data/manifests/build/`,
  as `explore/0006_feasibility.py` does. For close pairs, reuse
  `terrain.strongholds` exactly as that script's `load_election` does:
  `main_election`, `candidacy_votes`, `within_list_pairs`, `valid_votes`,
  `margins`, `close_pairs`.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

Exposure here is the **geology** exposure: `geo_exposed` in
`gold_exposure.parquet`. Every count is over exploration-half
municipalities.

- `exposure.n_munis_exposed` (int): municipalities with `geo_exposed`.
  **Kill rule k1.**
- `exposure.n_states_10plus` (int): states with at least 10 of them.
  **Kill rule k2.**
- `exposure.top_state_share` (float): the largest state's count over
  `n_munis_exposed`, or 1.0 if that is 0. **Kill rule k4.**
- `exposure.top_state` (str), `exposure.by_state` (object: state to count).
- `exposure.n_munis_title_exposed` (int): municipalities with
  `title_exposed`.
- `exposure.n_munis_both` (int): municipalities with both.
- `exposure.n_munis_half` (int): exploration-half municipalities in
  `gold_exposure`.
- `titles.share_dated` (float): sum of `title_n_gold_dated` over sum of
  `title_n_gold`, or 0.0 if the sum is 0. **Kill rule k3.**
- `titles.n_pre2000` (int), `titles.n_garimpo_pre2000` (int): sums of
  `title_n_gold_pre2000` and `title_n_garimpo_pre2000`. A title spanning
  two municipalities counts in both; say so in `summary.md`.
- `pairs.n_050_exposed` (int): close council pairs, margin at most 0.50%,
  in exposed municipalities, elections 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024,
  pooled. `pairs.n_050_exposed_by_year` (object: `y2008` .. `y2024`).
  Informational, for question 2.
- `candidates.n_mining_occupation` (object: `y2000` .. `y2024`): council
  candidates (`cd_cargo` 13) whose `cd_ocupacao` is 605 (GARIMPEIRO) or 253
  (TRABALHADOR DE MINAS E PEDREIRAS...).
  - `candidates.n_desc_garimpeiro` (same keys): candidates whose
    `ds_ocupacao`, accent-stripped and upper-cased, contains "GARIMPEIRO".
    This is a check that code 605 means the same in every year.
  - Informational; pooled; not split by exposure.
- `price.n_years` (int), `price.first_year`, `price.last_year`;
  `price.min_real` and `price.max_real` (float, 2024 USD per troy ounce);
  `price.min_year` and `price.max_year`. Use `gold_prices.parquet`, years
  1985-2025.
- `estimates.finite` (bool): true when every float above is finite.

`summary.md`: one short table per group, saying for each number what it
counts. Add a line that no outcome data was read.

## Out of scope

- Any regression, effect or balance test; any MapBiomas, SIM or outcome
  table; the confirmation half; `terrain.registered`.

## Done when

1. A test builds, in memory:
   - a small `gold_exposure` (two states, mixed exposure);
   - a `gold_prices` table;
   - a candidates table;
   - a two-pair results table.
   
   It runs the script's logic and checks every count above. It also
   checks that `results.json` has the four kill-rule fields at
   `exposure.n_munis_exposed`, `exposure.n_states_10plus`,
   `titles.share_dated` and `exposure.top_state_share`, each a number.
2. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before counting (test).
3. With no exposed municipality, `top_state_share` is 1.0 and
   `share_dated` is a number, not an error.
4. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
