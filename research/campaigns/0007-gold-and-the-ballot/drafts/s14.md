# Task: first stage: does the gold price move garimpo where gold lies? (explore, idea 0007)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0007_gold_first_stage.py`, on the
exploration half. It estimates the first stage of idea 0007, its event
study, placebos and legal-regime split, with the inference fixed below. Its
`results.json` carries the three fields that step s15's kill rules read.
Campaign `research/campaigns/0007-gold-and-the-ballot/campaign.md`, step
s14. Step s15 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0007 (`research/ideas/0007-gold-and-the-ballot.md`); decisions 0003 and
0011. This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may change only the
two files below.

## In scope

- `explore/0007_gold_first_stage.py` (new).
- `tests/test_explore_gold_first_stage.py` (new).

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store,
  national=True)`, then `restrict` every table on `cd_ibge` before anything
  else.
- **Data read (referee R2).** It reads only:
  - `mining_panel` (derived), outcome years 1990-2024;
  - `gold_exposure` (both files): `gold_exposure.parquet` and
    `gold_prices.parquet`, price years 1989-2023 (the t-1 lag).
  
  It reads no election data, no SIM data and nothing in `sealed/`.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** only the estimates listed
  below; no other outcome, window or exposure.
- **Compute:** `numpy` and `polars` only (there is no `scipy`,
  `statsmodels` or `pyfixest`). Write the fixed-effects regression with
  alternating projections (demean by municipality, then by state × year,
  repeated until the largest change is below 1e-10, at most 1,000 rounds).
  Partial out the controls by least squares on the demeaned data
  (Frisch-Waugh-Lovell). `explore/0006_feasibility.py` has `demean`,
  `partial_out` and `fwl_beta` as a model. The run must finish well inside
  `explore-run`'s 180 minutes.

### The design (fixed)

- **Sample:**
  - exploration-half municipalities with `n_cells` ≥ 1 and non-null
    controls;
  - years t = 1990..2024, a balanced panel.
  - Report the municipalities dropped and why.
- **Exposure E_m (main):** `geo_gold_per_km2`.
- **Shock:** `ln_gold_real` of year t-1.
- **Main outcome:** `garimpo_share` (0 to 1).
- **Regressor:** D_mt = E_m × ln_gold_real_{t-1}.
- **Fixed effects:** municipality, and state × year (`uf` from
  `gold_exposure`).
- **Controls X_m × year dummies:**
  - `native_share_2004` (mining_panel);
  - ln(`area_km2`);
  - `indigenous_share`;
  - each interacted with a full set of year dummies (one dropped).
- **Main coefficient:** β on D_mt.
  - `se_cluster`: municipality-clustered, with the small-sample factor
    G/(G-1) × (N-1)/(N-K). K counts the regressor and the partialled
    controls, not the absorbed fixed effects.
  - `f_cluster = (β / se_cluster)²`, the cluster-robust F for one
    regressor.
- **Conley standard errors, at 50, 100 and 200 km:**
  - Distances between municipality centroids (`centroid_x_km`,
    `centroid_y_km`), Euclidean in Albers km.
  - With s_m the municipality's score, the sum over its years of the
    residualised regressor × residual, the variance is
    Σ_m Σ_m' 1[d(m, m') ≤ c] s_m s_m' / (Σ residualised regressor²)².
  - The kernel is uniform and covers all years. This is municipality
    clustering extended to every municipality within c.
- **Randomisation inference (RI):**
  - 1,000 draws, seed 20261111.
  - Each draw permutes E_m across municipalities within the same state and
    re-estimates β with everything else fixed.
  - `ri_p = (1 + #{|β_draw| ≥ |β|}) / (1 + 1000)`.
- **Event study:**
  - Replace D_mt by E_m × 1[year = y] for y = 1990..2024 except 2000 (the
    base), with the same fixed effects and controls.
  - Coefficients b_y with clustered standard errors.
  - `placebo.lead_ratio = |mean(b_y, y in 1990..2001, y ≠ 2000)| /
    |mean(b_y, y in 2002..2024)|`. If the denominator is 0, write
    1000000000.0 (a fixed sentinel, so the JSON stays standard); the kill
    rule then fires.
- **Secondary outcomes,** same design as the main one, each with β,
  `se_cluster` and `ri_p`:
  - `garimpo_gold_share` (`garimpo_gold_ha / (n_cells × 100)`);
  - ln(1 + `garimpo_ha`);
  - `industrial_gold_share`;
  - `garimpo_other_share`.
- **Robustness exposures,** main outcome:
  - E_m = `title_gold_pre2000_share` (active titles only: survivors);
  - E_m = `geo_exposed` (binary).
- **Placebos,** main outcome:
  - *price*: E_m × `ln_soy_real` (t-1), in place of gold;
  - *exposure*: `title_sand_pre2000_share` × ln_gold_real (t-1).
- **Legal regimes (secondary; report, do not claim):**
  - Replace D_mt by D_mt × R_k, with R1 years 1990-2013, R2 2014-2022 and
    R3 2023-2024. One coefficient per regime, plus the difference R2 - R1
    with its clustered standard error.
  - R3 has two years only.
- **Legal access (secondary):**
  - Add D_mt × `geo_gold_indigenous_share` (0 where `geo_n_gold` is 0).
  - Report the interaction coefficient.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

- `first_stage.beta`, `first_stage.se`, `first_stage.f_cluster` (**kill
  rule k1**), `first_stage.ri_p`.
- `first_stage.conley_se_50km`, `first_stage.conley_se_100km`,
  `first_stage.conley_se_200km`.
- `first_stage.n_munis`, `first_stage.n_obs`, `first_stage.n_states`.
- `event.coef` and `event.se` (objects keyed `y1990` .. `y2024`, without
  `y2000`).
- `placebo.lead_ratio` (**kill rule k2**), `placebo.soy_beta`,
  `placebo.soy_se`, `placebo.sand_beta`, `placebo.sand_se`.
- `secondary.<name>.beta`, `.se`, `.ri_p` for `garimpo_gold_share`,
  `ln_garimpo_ha`, `industrial_gold_share` and `garimpo_other_share`.
- `robust.titles.beta`, `.se`, `.f_cluster`; `robust.binary.beta`, `.se`,
  `.f_cluster`.
- `regimes.r1.beta` and `.se`, the same for `r2` and `r3`;
  `regimes.r2_minus_r1.beta` and `.se`.
- `access.beta`, `access.se`.
- `price.mean_ln_gold_real`, `exposure.mean_geo_gold_per_km2`: sample
  means, for magnitudes.
- `estimates.finite` (bool, **kill rule k3**): true only if every number
  above was finite when computed. A non-finite number (NaN or infinity) is
  written as `null`, never as `NaN` or `Infinity`, so `results.json` stays
  standard JSON. The lead-ratio sentinel 1000000000.0 also sets this flag
  to false.

`summary.md`:

- the main estimate with its three kinds of standard errors and the RI p;
- the event-study coefficients next to the mean `ln_gold_real` of each year
  (as a table, no figure);
- one line per other block.

It says that the clustered errors can over-reject with one common shock
(Moreno-Louzada, Figueira and Picchetti 2025), so the RI p comes first.

## Out of scope

- Election outcomes, homicides, close races; any other window, lag or
  outcome; the confirmation half; `terrain.registered`.

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test recovers β within 10% and gives
   `f_cluster` above 10:
   - 60 municipalities in 3 states, 1990-2024;
   - outcome = 0.02 × E × lnP + municipality and state-year effects +
     noise.
   
   With the outcome made independent of E × lnP, the RI p is above 0.05,
   for that fixed seed.
2. The alternating projections match a dummy-variable regression to 1e-8
   on a small panel.
3. The Conley SE at a cutoff below the smallest distance equals the
   clustered SE (before the small-sample factor).
4. `results.json` has `first_stage.f_cluster`, `placebo.lead_ratio` and
   `estimates.finite` at those paths. A NaN in any estimate is written as
   `null` and sets `estimates.finite` to false. The file parses with
   `json.loads(text, parse_constant=...)` set to reject `NaN` and
   `Infinity`.
5. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before estimation (test).
6. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
