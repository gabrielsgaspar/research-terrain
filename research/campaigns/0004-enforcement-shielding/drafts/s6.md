# Task: coverage, counts, power and placebo of IBAMA enforcement near close-pair strongholds, pre-election years only (explore, idea 0005)

## Goal

A script, `explore/0005_enforcement_power.py`, that says whether the
enforcement outcome can be studied at all before any post-election year is
read. It measures, on the exploration half and **pre-election years
only**:

- how well IBAMA records are located;
- how many actions fall near close-pair strongholds;
- the minimum detectable effect at the 0.25% and 0.50% windows;
- the pre-election placebo at 0.50%.

Campaign `research/campaigns/0004-enforcement-shielding/campaign.md`, step
s6. Step s7 runs the script once through `explore-run`. Its four kill
rules read `coverage.elections_ok`, `counts.pre_reach_actions`,
`power.mde_share_025` and `placebo.ri_p_050`.

## Idea

The script serves idea 0005 (`research/ideas/0005-enforcement-shielding.md`)
and rests on:

- decision 0004 (exposure: exp(-d/2 km) to 10 km, winner minus loser,
  recentred as D / 2);
- decision 0005 (randomisation inference by re-flipping pairs,
  municipality-by-election fixed effects);
- decision 0007 (the MDE and the effect as a share of baseline);
- decision 0008 (windows; it computed pre-election power at 0.25% on this
  half, which is what this script does for a new outcome).

The data is `cell_enforcement` (`data/contracts/cell_enforcement.md`, step
s4). This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may change only
`explore/` and `tests/test_explore*`.

## In scope

- `explore/0005_enforcement_power.py` (new)
- `tests/test_explore_enforcement_power.py` (new)

Nothing else. The script calls `exploration_municipalities` and `restrict`
like every explore script, and writes through `write_results`.

### Code it reuses (loaded, not copied)

`explore/0001_window.py`'s per-window power, as `window_power` does it for
the **direct** estimate only:

- pairs filtered by `window_mask` at threshold 0.0025 or 0.005;
- `reached_cells`, `pb.pair_terms(..., "exp2")`;
- `pw.make_part`, `pw.assemble`, `pw.run_ri` and `pw.exposure_cells`;
- the stronghold pieces of `terrain.strongholds`.

These come from `explore/0001_window.py`, `explore/0001_power.py` and
`explore/0001_placebo.py`, loaded with `importlib` as `0001_window.py`
loads its neighbours. `election_inputs` in `0001_window.py` reads
`cell_outcomes`, so **this script must not call it**. It assembles the same
treatment inputs (`tse_results`, `section_votes`, `polling_places`,
`grid_cells`) from the same pieces and reads **no** `cell_outcomes`,
`polling_place_tenure` or `polling_place_types` file. It does not import
`terrain.registered`.

### Outcome data in scope (referee R2)

Only `cell_enforcement_<y>.parquet` and `muni_enforcement_<y>.parquet` of
the exploration half, for the years E-4 .. E-1 of each election E in
2008, 2012, 2016 and 2020: that is 2004-2019. No year E or later is ever
read. A function `pre_years(E)` fixes the years, and the reader is called
only with them. Cells reached by the kernel but absent from
`cell_enforcement` have zero actions.

The outcome Y of a cell for election E is
`n_fines_flora + n_embargoes`, summed over E-4 .. E-1.

### What `results.json` holds (exactly these keys; no key contains a dot)

- `coverage.e2008.share_geolocated`, `coverage.e2012.share_geolocated`,
  `coverage.e2016.share_geolocated`, `coverage.e2020.share_geolocated`.
  - For election E: the sum of `n_fines_flora_located + n_embargoes_located`
    divided by the sum of `n_fines_flora_all + n_embargoes_all`.
  - Taken over `muni_enforcement` of every exploration-half Legal Amazon
    municipality, years E-4 .. E-1.
  - It is the build's own-polygon check (step s4, rule 5), not the source's
    indicator. `null` if the denominator is 0.
- `coverage.elections_ok`: the number of those four elections whose share
  is at least 0.5 (an integer; a `null` share does not count).
- `counts.pre_reach_actions`: located pre-election actions (Y), summed
  over the cells with D ≠ 0 at the 0.50% window, pooled 2008-2020 (an
  integer). These are the exposed cells: the 10 km reach of the close
  pairs' strongholds, as decision 0007's baseline uses them.
- `power.mde_share_025` and `power.mde_share_050`, the minimum detectable
  effect at 80% power, 5% two-sided, as a share of baseline (0.25 means
  25%):
  - MDE slope = 2.8 × the SD of the direct slope over 2,000 re-flips of
    the window's close pairs (seed 20261106), on pre-election Y, with
    municipality-by-election fixed effects, pooled 2008-2020;
  - the share is that slope × mean |D| over the exposed cells, divided by
    mean Y over the same cells (decision 0007; `pw.exposure_cells` and
    `pct_effect` in `explore/0001_power.py`);
  - `null` if it cannot be computed. The rule then stops the step, which
    is intended.
- `placebo.ri_p_050`: the two-sided randomisation-inference p of the
  observed direct slope on pre-election Y at 0.50% against the same 2,000
  re-flips (`pw.ri_p`).

The script computes nothing beyond these keys (referee R7):

- no post-election quantity;
- no clearing;
- no estimate at 0.25% other than the MDE;
- no split by constituency type;
- no displacement;
- no clustered SE.

`summary.md` shows the same numbers and nothing else.

## Done when

1. A test builds a small synthetic exploration-half world inside the test:
   - two elections;
   - a few close pairs at margins inside 0.25%, and some between 0.25% and
     0.50%;
   - polling places and cells;
   - `cell_enforcement_<y>` and `muni_enforcement_<y>` frames, some for
     post-election years with values that would change every number.

   It runs the script's logic and checks that `results.json` has every key
   above at that exact dotted path, including the four the kill rules
   read. It also checks that the post-election frames were never requested
   by the reader.
2. `pre_years(2008) == [2004, 2005, 2006, 2007]`, and the reader raises if
   asked for a year ≥ E for election E.
3. `coverage.elections_ok` counts one election at share 0.5 and one at
   0.49 correctly. A zero denominator gives `null` and does not count.
4. `counts.pre_reach_actions` counts only cells with D ≠ 0 at 0.50%, and
   treats a reached cell absent from `cell_enforcement` as zero.
5. On a world with no effect, `placebo.ri_p_050` lies in (0, 1]. On a world
   whose pre-election Y follows D exactly, it is at most 0.05.
6. Each MDE share is a positive finite number on the synthetic world. A
   world whose 0.25% window has no pairs gives `null`.
7. The script does not import `terrain.registered` and reads no
   `cell_outcomes`: a test inspects the reads it makes and its imports.
8. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
