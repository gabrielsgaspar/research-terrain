# Task: post-election effect of a close council win on IBAMA flora fines and embargoes near the stronghold, at 0.50% (explore, idea 0005)

## Goal

A script, `explore/0005_enforcement_effects.py`, that estimates on the
exploration half, at the 0.50% window only, the effect of the
winner-minus-loser stronghold exposure on IBAMA enforcement per 1 km cell
in the four years after the election (t+1 to t+4). The pooled outcome is
flora fines plus embargoes (primary); fines and embargoes are also
estimated separately (secondary). The test is two-sided; enforcement
shielding predicts a negative sign.

Campaign `research/campaigns/0004-enforcement-shielding/campaign.md`, step
s8. It is filed only after step s7's pre-election checks (coverage, counts,
power, placebo) survived. Step s9 runs it once through `explore-run`; its
kill rule reads `estimates.finite`.

## Idea

The script serves idea 0005 (`research/ideas/0005-enforcement-shielding.md`)
and rests on:

- decision 0004 (exposure D, recentred as D / 2);
- decision 0005 (municipality-by-election fixed effects, randomisation
  inference by re-flipping close pairs);
- decision 0007 (the effect as slope × mean |D| over the baseline mean of
  the exposed cells);
- decision 0008 (0.50% is unbalanced on incumbency, so these estimates
  carry its caveat, as task B's did, run 42298466207).

The data is `cell_enforcement` (`data/contracts/cell_enforcement.md`).
This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may change only
`explore/` and `tests/test_explore*`.

## In scope

- `explore/0005_enforcement_effects.py` (new)
- `tests/test_explore_enforcement_effects.py` (new)

Nothing else. The script calls `exploration_municipalities` and `restrict`
and writes through `write_results`. It loads `explore/0005_enforcement_power.py`
(step s6) with `importlib` for the treatment inputs, the 0.50% pairs, the
exposure D and the re-flip machinery, and copies none of it. Like that
script, it:

- reads no `cell_outcomes`, `polling_place_tenure` or `polling_place_types`;
- does not import `terrain.registered`;
- fixes the window as a constant 0.005 and raises if asked for any other.

### Outcome data in scope (referee R2)

Only `cell_enforcement_<y>.parquet` of the exploration half, for each
election E in 2008, 2012, 2016 and 2020:

- years E+1 .. E+4 (the outcome);
- years E-4 .. E-1 (the baseline mean only).

No election uses its own year E. Exactly:

| E | baseline years | outcome years |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2008 | 2004-2007 | 2009-2012 |
| 2012 | 2008-2011 | 2013-2016 |
| 2016 | 2012-2015 | 2017-2020 |
| 2020 | 2016-2019 | 2021-2024 |

A year can be a baseline year of one election and an outcome year of
another; each election uses its own years. 2025 is never read.
`muni_enforcement` is not read. Cells reached by the kernel but absent from
`cell_enforcement` have zero actions.

### Estimates

Three outcomes per cell and election:

- `pooled` = `n_fines_flora + n_embargoes`
- `fines` = `n_fines_flora`
- `embargoes` = `n_embargoes`

Each is summed over E+1 .. E+4. For each outcome:

- `slope`: the coefficient on D / 2 in a regression with
  municipality-by-election fixed effects. Cells are those the `exp2`
  kernel reaches in municipalities with a close pair at 0.50%, as in the
  s6 script; elections 2008-2020 are pooled.
- `ri_p`: two-sided, against 2,000 re-flips of the close pairs (seed
  20261106), as `pw.ri_p`.
- `share`: slope × mean |D| over the exposed cells (D ≠ 0), divided by the
  mean of the same outcome summed over E-4 .. E-1 over the same cells
  (decision 0007). It is negative if winners' areas get less enforcement.

### What `results.json` holds (exactly these keys; no key contains a dot)

- `estimates.pooled.slope`, `estimates.pooled.share`, `estimates.pooled.ri_p`
- `estimates.fines.slope`, `estimates.fines.share`, `estimates.fines.ri_p`
- `estimates.embargoes.slope`, `estimates.embargoes.share`,
  `estimates.embargoes.ri_p`
- `estimates.finite`: the JSON boolean `true` if all nine numbers above
  are finite, `false` otherwise (never a number, never missing).

A non-finite value is written as `null`, never as NaN.

The script computes nothing beyond these keys (referee R7):

- no placebo (step s7 has it);
- no power;
- no 0.25% window;
- no other distance, kernel, window or year span;
- no clustered SE;
- no split by constituency type;
- no displacement;
- no clearing.

`summary.md` shows the same numbers, labelled "exploration half, 0.50%
window, not a finding; decision 0008's incumbency caveat applies".

## Done when

1. A test builds a small synthetic exploration-half world inside the test,
   with two elections, close pairs, cells and `cell_enforcement_<y>`
   frames:
   - each election's year E carries values that would change every
     estimate;
   - the outcome years carry Y = −c × D for a known c.

   The pooled slope recovers −c within 1e-9 and its share has the expected
   value. No election requests its own year E from the reader.
2. Results carry every key above at that exact dotted path.
   `estimates.finite` is the boolean `true` on that world, and `false` on
   a world with no exposed cell (shares `null`).
3. The baseline and outcome years per election equal the table above, and
   asking for an election's own year E, or for 2025, raises.
4. The window is 0.005; calling the estimator with 0.0025 raises.
5. The script loads `0005_enforcement_power.py` rather than copying it,
   does not import `terrain.registered` and reads no `cell_outcomes`: a
   test inspects its reads and imports.
6. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
