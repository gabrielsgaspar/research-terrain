# Task: build the deputy × municipality × cycle amendments panel, split by half (build, idea 0009)

## Goal

A new build, `amendments_panel`. It has one row per federal deputy,
municipality and cycle wherever the deputy's party had one of the two
finalists of a mayoral election. Each row holds the race, the deputy's
individual-amendment payments to the municipality before and after the
race, and the deputy's vote share there at the general elections before and
after. Exploration-half rows go to derived, all others only to sealed.

Campaign `research/campaigns/0009-amendments-ballot-box/campaign.md`, step
s4. Step s5 runs the build; steps s6 and s8 write the scripts that read it.

### Open questions and their answers (so the plan needs no clarification)

- *Which amendments?* Individual amendments only: `Tipo de Emenda`, compared
  upper-cased and accent-stripped, starts with `EMENDA INDIVIDUAL`.
  Bench, committee and rapporteur amendments have no single deputy author.
- *Paid when, and to whom?* By payment month (`Ano/Mês`) in
  `EmendasParlamentares_PorFavorecido.csv`, to any recipient whose
  `UF Favorecido` and `Município Favorecido` are the municipality.
- *Pix?* `Tipo de Emenda`, normalised as above, contains
  `TRANSFERENCIAS ESPECIAIS`.
- *Which deputy key?* The TSE `sq_candidato` of the deputy's base
  general-election candidacy (t0). No CPF, voter ID, name or hash of one is
  ever written.
- *Party across elections?* `nr_partido` (party number), not the acronym.

### Inputs (from `data/manifests/` and `data/catalog.yaml`)

- `tse_general` (raw, step s2): see `data/contracts/tse_general.md`. Read only
  the `*_BRASIL.csv` member of each zip; `;`-separated, quoted, Latin-1.
  Federal deputy is `CD_CARGO` 6 and senator 5. Votes: use
  `QT_VOTOS_NOMINAIS_VALIDOS` where the year has it (2018, 2022, 2026),
  else `QT_VOTOS_NOMINAIS` (2014). Rows with no TSE municipality in
  `municipalities` (votes abroad, `SG_UF` `ZZ`) are dropped and counted.
- `cgu_amendments` (raw, step s3): see `data/contracts/cgu_amendments.md`.
  Read `EmendasParlamentares_PorFavorecido.csv`. Its encoding and the
  format of `Ano/Mês` are unconfirmed:
  - accept UTF-8 or Latin-1;
  - accept `YYYY/MM`, `MM/YYYY` and `YYYYMM`;
  - stop if neither encoding or none of the formats works.
  - `Valor Recebido` is Brazilian (`1.234,56`).
  - Never read `Código do Favorecido` or `Favorecido` into a frame that is
    written anywhere.
- `tse_results` (derived): `candidate_votes_<year>.parquet` and
  `turnout_<year>.parquet` for 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024
  (`data/contracts/tse_results.md`). Mayor is `cd_cargo` 11.
- `tse_candidates` (derived): `cd_genero`, `dt_nascimento`,
  `cd_grau_instrucao` and `nr_partido`, keyed by (`ano_eleicao`,
  `cd_eleicao`, `sg_ue`, `sq_candidato`).
- `municipalities` (derived): `cd_ibge`, `cd_tse`, `uf`, `name_ibge`.
- `ibge_population_annual` (raw): `ano`, `id_municipio`, `populacao`.
- The two exploration lists.

### Deputies and the author link (in memory)

1. **Deputies of a cycle:** the t0 `CD_CARGO` 6 candidacies that were
   elected (`CD_SIT_TOT_TURNO` 1, 2 or 3), plus any other t0 `CD_CARGO` 6
   candidacy matched as an author below with a payment anywhere in the
   cycle's pre or post window (a substitute who served).
2. **Names:** normalise by upper-casing, stripping accents, keeping letters
   and spaces, and collapsing spaces.
3. **Matching:** match each CGU author (`Código do Autor da Emenda`, with its
   `Nome do Autor da Emenda`) paying in the cycle's windows against
   `NM_URNA_CANDIDATO` and `NM_CANDIDATO` of the t0 `CD_CARGO` 6 candidacies.
   - Exactly one candidacy matches: deputy.
   - More than one: ambiguous, so unmatched.
4. **Senators:** an unmatched author that matches exactly one elected senate
   candidacy (`CD_CARGO` 5, codes 1-3) at t0 or t0−4 (2010 for cycle A) is a
   senator. Senators have no row.
5. **Author link counts:** per cycle, authors in total, deputies, senators,
   ambiguous and unmatched. They go to `author_link.parquet` (counts only)
   and to the manifest notes.
6. **t0 to t1:** link each deputy's t0 candidacy to a t1 `CD_CARGO` 6
   candidacy in the same state by `NR_CPF_CANDIDATO`, in memory only. A CPF
   that is empty or not 11 digits is unlinked; count those per cycle in the
   notes.

### Recipient municipality

Match (`UF Favorecido`, normalised `Município Favorecido`) to
(`uf`, normalised `name_ibge`).

- Rows with an empty municipality (state or federal recipients) are dropped
  and counted per year.
- Stop if more than 2% of the rows that do name a municipality fail to
  match, per year.

### Rows: one per `cycle` × `deputy` × `cd_ibge`

Keep only municipalities in the deputy's t0 state whose mayoral deciding
round at m had a finalist with the deputy's t0 `nr_partido`. Use the
deciding round (round 2 where there was one), as in
`explore/0003_mayor_power.py`. The finalists are the top two by votes.

Columns:

- `cycle`, `m`, `t0`, `t1`, `cd_ibge`, `uf`.
- `deputy` (text: t0 `sq_candidato`), `deputy_party` (t0 `nr_partido`),
  `deputy_elected` (bool).
- `population_m`: `populacao` in year m.
- `round` (1 or 2), `n_mayor_cands` (round 1).
- `margin`: (votes of the deputy's party's finalist − the other finalist's)
  / valid votes of the deciding round. `win` = `margin` > 0.
- Balance covariates of the deputy's party's finalist:
  - `fin_female`: `cd_genero` "4";
  - `fin_age`: whole years from `dt_nascimento` to the election date; null
    if the date is null;
  - `fin_higher_ed`: `cd_grau_instrucao` "8".
  - Stop if, in a year, no mayoral candidate has code "4" or no candidate
    has code "8".
- `party_held_mayor`: the deputy's party won the mayoralty at m−4 (from
  `tse_results`, same deciding-round rule).
- Payments in reais, nominal; 0 where none:
  - `amend_pre_total`, `amend_pre_pix`, `n_pay_pre` (the pre window);
  - `amend_post_total`, `amend_post_pix`, `n_pay_post` (the post window).
- Votes at t0:
  - `votes_t0`: the deputy's votes in the municipality;
  - `valid_t0`: the sum of all `CD_CARGO` 6 candidacies' votes there;
  - `share_t0` = `votes_t0` / `valid_t0`.
- Votes at t1:
  - `ran_t1` (bool): linked to a t1 candidacy with statewide votes above 0;
  - `votes_t1`, `valid_t1`, `share_t1`;
  - `d_share` = `share_t1` − `share_t0`;
  - all null when `ran_t1` is false.

### Outputs

- `panel_<cycle>.parquet`: exploration-half rows (both lists) in derived,
  all other rows only in sealed, as `politics_panel`.
- `author_link.parquet` (derived only): one row per cycle with the counts
  of item 5 above. No author code or name.

### Checks that stop the build

- A duplicate (`cycle`, `deputy`, `cd_ibge`).
- `margin` outside [−1, 1], or `win` not equal to `margin` > 0.
- Shares outside [0, 1]; a payment below 0.
- Fewer than 5,000 or more than 300,000 rows in a cycle.
- The earliest payment month is after 2015-01, or the latest is before
  2026-09.
- No Pix row from 2020 on.
- The recipient-matching rule above.

Manifest notes give per cycle:

- rows and deputies;
- the author link counts and unlinked CPFs;
- dropped recipient rows;
- t0 deputies whose party number has no mayoral candidate in m (mergers).

Also the first and last payment months.
