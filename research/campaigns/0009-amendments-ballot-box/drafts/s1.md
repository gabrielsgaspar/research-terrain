# Task: catalog entries, contracts and acquire scripts for TSE general elections and CGU amendments (build, idea 0009)

## Goal

Two new datasets can be acquired by the generic acquirer
(`terrain.acquire`), with no new acquire logic:

- `tse_general`: TSE general-election votes by candidate and municipality,
  and candidacies;
- `cgu_amendments`: the CGU Portal da Transparência amendment zip.

Campaign `research/campaigns/0009-amendments-ballot-box/campaign.md`, step
s1. Steps s2 and s3 run the two acquires; step s4 writes the build that
reads them.

## Idea

Idea 0009 (`research/ideas/0009-amendments-ballot-box.md`); decision 0011
(national scope). Models: the `tse_results` and `tse_candidates` entries in
`data/catalog.yaml` (generic `acquire.files` with `<year>` and a zip
check), and `pipeline/acquire/tse_votes_by_section.py` (a thin script). This
is a **build** task: it may change exactly the six files below.

### Catalog entry `tse_general`

- `source`: "TSE open data (cdn.tse.jus.br), general elections:
  votacao_candidato_munzona 2014-2026 and consulta_cand 2010-2026".
- `licence`: "TSE open data: CC BY (dadosabertos.tse.jus.br)".
- `gcs: data/raw/tse/general/`; `status: planned`;
  `contract: data/contracts/tse_general.md`.
- `acquire.files`:
  - `https://cdn.tse.jus.br/estatistica/sead/odsele/votacao_candidato_munzona/votacao_candidato_munzona_<year>.zip`,
    `years: [2014, 2018, 2022, 2026]`, `check: {type: zip, member: "*_BRASIL.csv"}`;
  - `https://cdn.tse.jus.br/estatistica/sead/odsele/consulta_cand/consulta_cand_<year>.zip`,
    `years: [2010, 2014, 2018, 2022, 2026]`, same check.
- `notes` (facts checked on 2026-11-15 from the zips' directories and
  header rows, with no data row read):
  - Each zip has 30 members: one CSV per state (27, DF included), `_BR`,
    `_BRASIL` (every row again) and `leiame.pdf`. Read only `*_BRASIL.csv`.
  - Zipped sizes: munzona 494, 395, 577 and 454 MB (2014, 2018, 2022,
    2026); 7.4 to 8.6 GB unzipped each; `consulta_cand` 3-5 MB each.
  - CSVs are `;`-separated, quoted, Latin-1.
  - Munzona columns include `ANO_ELEICAO`, `NR_TURNO`, `SG_UF`,
    `CD_MUNICIPIO` (TSE code), `CD_CARGO`, `SQ_CANDIDATO`, `NR_PARTIDO`,
    `SG_PARTIDO`, `QT_VOTOS_NOMINAIS` and `CD_SIT_TOT_TURNO`. 2018, 2022
    and 2026 also have `NR_FEDERACAO`, `QT_VOTOS_NOMINAIS_VALIDOS` and
    `NM_TIPO_DESTINACAO_VOTOS`. 2014 has neither of the last two.
  - `consulta_cand` columns include `SQ_CANDIDATO`, `CD_CARGO`, `SG_UF`,
    `NM_CANDIDATO`, `NM_URNA_CANDIDATO`, `NR_PARTIDO`, `SG_PARTIDO`,
    `NR_CPF_CANDIDATO`, `NR_TITULO_ELEITORAL_CANDIDATO` and
    `CD_SIT_TOT_TURNO`.
  - The raw zips hold CPF and voter-ID numbers as TSE publishes them. They
    are stored as fetched, like `tse_candidates`' raw zips. Builds may use
    them only in memory (`AGENTS.md`, "Personal identifiers").
  - The 2026 files were regenerated on 2026-11-15 and can change with court
    rulings; the dated raw folder is the snapshot.
  - Fallback: Base dos Dados `br_tse_eleicoes`.
  - Campaign 0009 steps s1 and s2.

### Catalog entry `cgu_amendments`

- `source`: "CGU Portal da Transparência (dadosabertos-download.cgu.gov.br),
  EmendasParlamentares.zip".
- `licence`: "Portal da Transparência open data (federal open-data policy);
  licence not stated by the publisher, accepted by Gabriel on 2026-11-15 for
  research use".
- `gcs: data/raw/cgu/amendments/`; `status: planned`;
  `contract: data/contracts/cgu_amendments.md`.
- `acquire.files`: one file,
  `https://dadosabertos-download.cgu.gov.br/PortalDaTransparencia/saida/emendas-parlamentares/EmendasParlamentares.zip`,
  `check: {type: zip, member: "EmendasParlamentares_PorFavorecido.csv"}`.
  This is the target of the 302 redirect from
  `portaldatransparencia.gov.br/download-de-dados/emendas-parlamentares/UNICO`.
  It sends a `Content-Length` (47,094,995 bytes on 2026-11-13).
- `notes`:
  - Six CSVs (`;`-separated, quoted, Latin-1 to be confirmed by the build):
    - `EmendasParlamentares.csv` (49 MB): one row per amendment, with
      `Código da Emenda`, `Ano da Emenda`, `Tipo de Emenda`,
      `Código do Autor da Emenda`, `Nome do Autor da Emenda`,
      `Código Município IBGE`, the budget classification, and the
      committed, liquidated and paid values;
    - `EmendasParlamentares_PorFavorecido.csv` (184 MB): `Código da Emenda`,
      `Código do Autor da Emenda`, `Nome do Autor da Emenda`,
      `Número da emenda`, `Tipo de Emenda`, `Ano/Mês`,
      `Código do Favorecido`, `Favorecido`, `Natureza Jurídica`,
      `Tipo Favorecido`, `UF Favorecido`, `Município Favorecido`,
      `Valor Recebido`;
    - `_Convenios`, `_Parcerias`, `_PlanodeTrabalhoParcerias` and
      `_PlanodeTrabalhoTransferenciasEspeciais` (not used).
  - `Código do Favorecido` and `Favorecido` can identify people. No build
    may write them to derived data, logs or results.
  - The CGU guide says amendments are covered from 2014. The first
    `Ano/Mês` is unconfirmed; step s4's build checks it.
  - No GitHub runner has fetched from this host before (campaign 0009, step
    s3, is the first).
  - Fallbacks: Base dos Dados
    `basedosdados.br_cgu_emendas_parlamentares.microdados` (budget years
    2013-2026, no payment date); Tesouro Transparente
    `emendas-parlamentares.csv` (payment orders, with dates and a
    `Transferência Especial` flag, but no author code).
