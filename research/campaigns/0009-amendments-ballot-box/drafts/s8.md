# Task: what amendments buy: first stage and reduced form in close mayoral races (explore, idea 0009)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0009_amendments_effects.py`, on the
exploration half. It estimates idea 0009's first stage and reduced form,
with the secondary analyses declared in the campaign. Its `results.json`
carries `first_stage.f_cluster` and `estimates.finite`, the fields step
s9's kill rules read. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0009-amendments-ballot-box/campaign.md`, step s8. Step
s9 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0009 (`research/ideas/0009-amendments-ballot-box.md`); decisions 0003,
0008 and 0011. Step s6's script, `explore/0009_amendments_gate.py`, holds
the window choice (`choose_window`), the draws (seed 20261115), the model
and the clustered errors. Load it with the `_load` pattern of
`explore/0006_effects.py` and reuse them unchanged, so h is the window s7
chose. This is an **explore** task (label `explore`): it may change only
the two files below.

## In scope

- `explore/0009_amendments_effects.py` (new).
- `tests/test_explore_amendments_effects.py` (new).

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store,
  national=True)`, then `restrict` on `cd_ibge` before anything else.
- **Data read (referee R2).** Only `amendments_panel` (derived), `panel_A`,
  `panel_B` and `panel_C`. The outcomes are the point of this step and the
  campaign lists them:
  - payments in the post window (January m+1 to September m+2;
    `amend_post_total`, `amend_post_pix`);
  - votes at t1 = 2018, 2022 and 2026 (`votes_t1`, `share_t1`, `d_share`,
    `ran_t1`).

  Nothing in `sealed/`.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** exactly the estimates below,
  at h and at 5%; no other window, outcome or subgroup.
- **Compute:** as step s6's script.

### The design (fixed)

- **Units, model, draws and inference** as in s6's script:
  - Y = b·win + g·margin + d·win·margin + deputy-by-cycle fixed effects, on
    |margin| ≤ w;
  - standard errors clustered by `cd_ibge`;
  - race re-flips, 2,000 draws, seed 20261115;
  - `ri_p` = (1 + #{|b_draw| ≥ |b|}) / 2001.
- **h:** `choose_window` from s6's script. If it finds no window, raise:
  s7 would have stopped the campaign.
- **Windows:** every estimate at h (keys without suffix) and at 5% (under
  `at_5pct`).
- **First stage (kill rule k1):**
  - Y = `amend_post_total` / `population_m` × 1,000.
  - Write b, se, `ri_p` and the outcome mean.
  - `f_cluster` = (b / se)².
  - Variants, same model:
    - asinh(Y);
    - any > 0 (1 if `amend_post_total` > 0);
    - for cycles B and C only: Pix per 1,000 (`amend_post_pix`) and other
      per 1,000 (`amend_post_total` − `amend_post_pix`).
- **Reduced form:** Y = `d_share` (the change in the deputy's vote share in
  the municipality, t1 minus t0), on units with `ran_t1` true. Write b, se,
  `ri_p` and the outcome mean. Also report the count of units with
  `ran_t1` false.
- **By cycle (secondary):**
  - the model with `win`, `margin` and `win·margin` each interacted with
    cycle dummies;
  - b for cycles A, B and C, for the first stage and the reduced form;
  - the C-minus-A difference with its clustered SE.
- **Wald ratio (secondary; an upper bound, because allied mayors also
  campaign):**
  - b of Y = `votes_t1` − `votes_t0` (units with `ran_t1`) divided by b of
    Y = `amend_post_total` (reais, all units);
  - times 1,000: votes per 1,000 reais.
  - Report it, its two parts, and the label "upper bound" in
    `summary.md`. No standard error.
- **Split at 10,000 inhabitants (secondary):** the first stage and the
  reduced form on `population_m` < 10,000 and on ≥ 10,000 separately.

### `results.json` keys (exact; no dots inside a key)

- `window.h_pct`.
- `first_stage.f_cluster` (float, **k1**), `first_stage.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`,
  `.mean`, `.n_units`, `.n_races`;
  `first_stage.asinh.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`;
  `first_stage.any.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`;
  `first_stage.pix.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`, `.mean`;
  `first_stage.other.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`, `.mean`.
- `reduced_form.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`, `.mean`, `.n_units`,
  `.n_not_running`.
- `by_cycle.first_stage.<A|B|C>.b` and `.se`;
  `by_cycle.first_stage.c_minus_a.b` and `.se`; the same under
  `by_cycle.reduced_form`.
- `wald.votes_per_1000_reais`, `wald.rf_votes_b`, `wald.fs_reais_b`.
- `split.small.first_stage.b` and `.se`; `split.small.reduced_form.b` and
  `.se`; the same under `split.large`.
- `at_5pct.first_stage.f_cluster`, `at_5pct.first_stage.b`, `.se`,
  `.ri_p`; `at_5pct.reduced_form.b`, `.se`, `.ri_p`.
- `estimates.finite` (bool, **k2**): true only if every number above was
  finite when computed.

Non-finite numbers are written as `null` and set `estimates.finite` to false.
`first_stage.f_cluster` is never null: if it cannot be computed, raise.

`summary.md`:

- one table: estimate, b, SE, RI p, mean, at h and at 5%;
- the by-cycle table;
- the Wald ratio, labelled an upper bound;
- a line that nothing on the exploration half is a finding.

## Out of scope

- Coalitions or federations, state deputies, Pix-only samples beyond the
  two lines above, any window other than h and 5%, the confirmation half.

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test recovers planted effects within 10%:
   - three cycles, 300 races;
   - a planted first-stage jump and a planted reduced-form jump;
   - `f_cluster` above 10 with the jump, and RI p above 0.05 for a null
     outcome with the fixed seed.
2. The by-cycle C-minus-A difference matches the difference of the planted
   cycle effects within 10%.
3. Units with `ran_t1` false are excluded from the reduced form and
   counted.
4. `results.json` has `first_stage.f_cluster` as a number and
   `estimates.finite`, and parses as standard JSON.
5. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before estimation (test).
6. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
