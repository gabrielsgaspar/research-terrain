# f1: The incumbency gate with a uniform kernel at 10%

## Why (from the ideation job)

Run 45246860634 passed every s2 rule at the triangular h = 0.135. Its decision-0008 table (information only) gives, for the uniform 10% window on all rows, omnibus p 0.0185 and min p 0.0045 (fin_female), and at 7.5% omnibus p 0.034. That table uses four covariates including the lagged outcome, not the gate's five predetermined covariates, and it has no placebo with the size condition and no power. So it cannot say whether the uniform 10% design, one of s4's robustness fits and a variant the campaign file names, passes the gate's own tests. The fork re-runs the gate's tests with that one change. It reuses mayor_incumbency_panel and the gate's own functions, so it needs one explore task and one run and no new data.

## What differs from the parent

kernel and bandwidth: uniform kernel with |margin| <= 0.10 instead of triangular with h = 0.135, in every gate fit (counts, balance, placebo, power, all rows and the S2 sample)

## What a pass or a fail would mean

Pass (survived, fork): the uniform 10% design is also balanced on the five predetermined covariates, shows no lagged-outcome jump, has an MDE within the rule and has enough races per cycle. The close-out can then read s4's uniform 10% robustness fit as resting on a design as valid as the main one. Fail (killed, fork): at 10% uniform, balance (most likely fin_female) or another s2 rule fails. The close-out must then say that s4's uniform 10% robustness fit stands on an unbalanced design and give it less weight, and it must not recommend that design for the confirmation half. Either way it is a further look at the same exploration half, not a finding, and the count of forks tried (1) is reported next to it.
