# Task: the incumbency curse, 2004-2020: H1 (2020 vs 2008-2016), S1-S5, robustness and five figures at h = 0.135 (explore, idea 0012)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0012_incumbency_main.py`, on the
exploration half. With the gate's design (triangular kernel, h = 0.135,
clustered by municipality, 2,000 race re-flips) it estimates idea 0012's
primary hypothesis H1, the secondary estimates S1 to S5 and the
robustness list, and draws five figures. Its `results.json` carries
`estimates.finite`, the field step s4's kill rule reads. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0014-incumbency-curse/campaign.md`, step s3. Step s4
runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0012 (`research/ideas/0012-incumbency-curse.md`); decisions 0017,
0003 and 0011. Step s1's script, `explore/0012_incumbency_gate.py`, holds
the reading, the races, the draws (seed 20261122), the kernel weights,
the weighted model (`fit`) and the randomisation draws (`ri_draws`). Load
it with the `_load` pattern of `explore/0006_effects.py` and reuse those
functions unchanged (their names and arguments are listed in s1's
issue, `research/campaigns/0014-incumbency-curse/drafts/s1.md`,

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national:** `exploration_municipalities(store,
  national=True)`, then `restrict` on `cd_ibge` before anything else.
- **Data read (referee R2).** Only `mayor_incumbency_panel` (derived),
  `panel_2004.parquet` ... `panel_2020.parquet`, found as s1's script
  finds them, nothing in `sealed/`. Columns: s1's `READ_COLUMNS`, plus
  these, which are the point of this step:
  - outcomes `party_wins_next`, `party_runs_next`, `finalist_wins_next`,
    `finalist_runs_next`, `party_next`;
  - `amend_after_total`, `amend_after_pix` and `population_e`.

  The outcome years are 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020 and 2024 (elections at
  e+4), and for the money January of e+1 to September of e+4.
- **The design is fixed (idea 0012).** h = 0.135, triangular kernel, from
  s1's constants; never chosen from the data. The robustness fits use
  only the bandwidths listed below.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** exactly the estimates below.

### Facts about the data (`data/contracts/mayor_incumbency_panel.md`)

- Two rows per race, (`election`, `cd_ibge`). `win` = `margin` > 0.
- The four `*_next` columns are null where the municipality had no
  mayoral election at e+4 (2, 2 and 6 exploration-half rows in 2012,
  2016 and 2020 in PR #436's dry run). The finalist ones are also null
  where the finalist has neither CPF nor título (10 rows in 2012). A row
  with a null Y leaves that fit only; count those rows.
- `finalist_wins_next` is false when the finalist did not run at e+4.
- `party_next` is the party at e+4 after mergers, or `F<n>` for a 2024
  federation.
- `finalist_eligible`: the finalist had not won at e-4, so is not
  term-limited at e+4.
- `bench_fed` is an int, null for 2004 and 2008, 0 where the party
  elected no federal deputy in the state.
- `amend_after_total` and `amend_after_pix` are reais, null for 2004,
  2008 and 2012; they are the same for both rows of a race (municipality
  money). `population_e` is an int and can be null.

### The estimates (all declared in idea 0012)

Every estimate is s1's `fit` with the triangular kernel at h = 0.135
unless stated, with its clustered se, a 95% interval (b ± 1.96 × se), and
the RI p from s1's `ri_draws` (two-sided, (1 + #{|draw| ≥ |b|}) / 2001).

- **H1 (primary, alpha 0.05).** Y = `party_wins_next`, all rows. Groups
  pre = {2008, 2012, 2016}, post = {2020}. Report `b_pre`, `b_post`, and
  the difference b_post − b_pre with its clustered se (from the one
  fit's covariance), interval, clustered p and RI p. The summary states
  the rule: H1 holds on this half if the difference is positive and its
  RI p is below 0.05.
- **S1.** Y = `party_wins_next`, all rows: each election 2004-2020 as its
  own group (b, se, interval, RI p, mean of Y, units, races, null-Y rows);
  and 2004-2012 pooled as one group, the replication of Klašnja and
  Titiunik's -0.15.
- **S2, the personal effect.** Y = `finalist_wins_next`, rows with
  `finalist_eligible` true: the H1 difference (same groups) and each
  election 2004-2020.
- **S3.** Y = `party_runs_next` (all rows) and Y = `finalist_runs_next`
  (rows with `finalist_eligible` true): for each, the H1 difference and
  each election 2004-2020.
- **S4, connected parties.** Y = `party_wins_next`, all rows of 2012,
  2016 and 2020; `connected` = 1[`bench_fed` > 0].
  - Cell = election × connected (each cell its own intercept and slopes;
    pass a cell column to `fit`). Four `win` groups: pre-unconnected
    {2012, 2016} × 0, post-unconnected {2020} × 0, pre-connected, post-
    connected.
  - S4 = (b_post,conn − b_pre,conn) − (b_post,unconn − b_pre,unconn),
    with its clustered se, interval, clustered p and RI p.
  - Also the four b's, and for figure 4 each election × connected cell's
    own b, se and interval (one group per cell; no RI).
- **S5, town money.** One row per race:
  - races of 2016 and 2020 whose two rows have non-null, unequal
    `bench_fed`; keep the row with the larger `bench_fed` (the
    better-connected party). Count the races dropped for equal or null
    `bench_fed`.
  - Y_total = ln(1 + `amend_after_total` / `population_e`); Y_pix =
    ln(1 + `amend_after_pix` / `population_e`), 2020 only. Null where the
    money or the population is null, or the population is 0; those rows
    leave the fit and are counted.
  - Estimates: 2016 total, 2020 total, 2020 Pix (each election its own
    group), and 2020 minus 2016 for the total (one fit, groups {2016},
    {2020}), each with interval and RI p.
- **Robustness (all reported; H1 difference only).** b, clustered se,
  interval, clustered p, units, races; no RI:
  - `uniform_5pct`, `uniform_7_5pct`, `uniform_10pct`: uniform kernel at
    0.05, 0.075, 0.10;
  - `triangular_10pct`, `triangular_20pct`: triangular at 0.10, 0.20;
  - `controls_fe`: the design at h = 0.135 adding `fin_female`, `fin_age`,
    `fin_higher_ed` and state (`uf`) × election dummies, which replace the
    per-election intercepts (keep the per-election slopes). Rows with a
    null control leave this fit and are counted. Write this one fit in
    this script (weighted least squares, CR1 by `cd_ibge`, as s1's `fit`).
- **Federations.** The number of 2020 rows with |margin| < h whose
  `party_next` starts with `F`.

`summary.md`:

- the design (h, kernel, seed) and its source;
- the H1 table (b_pre, b_post, difference, se, interval, clustered p, RI
  p) and the rule it is judged by;
- the S1 to S5 and robustness tables;
- the figures, linked;
- the caveats:
  - the idea was formed after campaign 0012's gate and reuses idea 0011's
    data and this exploration half;
  - the 2020 election was held in the pandemic; company donations were
    banned from 2016 and the public campaign fund began in 2018;

  - 2024 federations count as one party;
  - nothing on the exploration half is a finding.

## Done when

1. A synthetic panel built in the test (five elections, at least 400
   races each) recovers, within 10%:
   - a planted H1 difference;
   - a planted S4 difference;
   - with RI p above 0.05 for a null outcome under the fixed seed.
2. S2 and `finalist_runs_next` use only `finalist_eligible` rows (test).
3. S5: a race with equal `bench_fed` is dropped and counted; the row kept
   is the larger `bench_fed`'s; a zero population gives a null Y that is
   counted (tests).
4. The `controls_fe` fit matches `pyfixest.feols` with `weights=` and
   `vcov={"CRV1": "cd_ibge"}` on the same rows and columns (b and se of
   the H1 difference within a relative 1e-6).
5. The robustness fits use exactly the six listed kernels and
   bandwidths (a test reads the labels).
6. Rows with a null Y leave only that fit and are counted.
7. Rows outside the exploration half are dropped before estimation
   (test).
8. The five figures are written, each a PNG under 400 KB, and
   `figures.count` is 5.
9. `results.json` carries `estimates.finite` and parses as standard JSON.
10. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run
    pytest` pass.
