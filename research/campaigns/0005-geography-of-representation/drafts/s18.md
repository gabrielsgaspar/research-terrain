estimate of idea 0006 on the exploration half, on **post-election** years:

- the primary outcome at three windows;
- the falsification on state schools;
- three secondary outcomes with Benjamini-Hochberg correction;
- one pre-set heterogeneity.

### Estimates (fixed; nothing else is computed)

Every regression is outcome on D (or the D terms), `S_p`, the stated
baseline and municipality-by-election fixed effects. Each reports:

- β (Frisch-Waugh);
- the RI p (2,000 draws, seed 20261110, the same flips for every estimate
  at a window; `ri_p` of `explore/0001_power.py`);
- the municipality-clustered SE (`clustered_se` of `explore/0001_placebo.py`);
- `n_places`, `n_pairs` and `mean_abs_d` (mean |D_p| over places with
  D_p ≠ 0).

1. **Primary.** At windows 0.50% (main), 0.25% and 1.00% (robustness).
   - Outcome: `school_n_fac` at E+4 minus at E; baseline `school_n_fac` at
     E.
   - Sample: municipal-school places (`school_rede` "3"), elections
     2008-2020.
   - Also `effect_sd` = β × mean |D_p| / sd_pre. sd_pre is the standard
     deviation (ddof 1) of `school_n_fac`(E) − `school_n_fac`(E-4) over
     municipal-school places of elections 2012-2020 at the same window,
     the denominator of step s16's MDE.
2. **Falsification**, 0.50% only. The same as the primary on state-school
   places (`school_rede` "2"), with its own `effect_sd` (sd_pre over
   state-school places). Expected near zero.
3. **Secondary**, 0.50% only, located places (`located_1km`):
   - a. `lights`: the mean of `lights_1km` over the years E+1 .. E+4 that
     exist, minus the mean over E-4 .. E-1. Baseline: the pre mean.
     Elections 2016 and 2020 only. For 2016 the pre years are 2013-2015,
     because there is no 2012 image.
   - b. `builtup`: `builtup_1km` at E+4 minus at E. Baseline: at E.
     Elections 2008-2020.
   - c. `native`: native-vegetation loss, `native_10km` at E minus at E+4,
     so positive means more cleared. Baseline: `native_10km` at E.
     Elections 2008-2020.
   
   `bh_p`: Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p-values of the three RI p-values,
   and `bh_reject_05` (bool each) at 0.05.
4. **Heterogeneity**, primary outcome and sample, 0.50% only.
   - Close pairs are split by whether the **winner's** party is in the
     elected mayor's coalition: `elected_mayor`, `mayor_coalition`,
     `is_aligned` and `council_parties` of `explore/0004_alignment_counts.py`,
     loaded with `importlib`.
   - `D_aligned`, `D_unaligned` and `D_unknown` are D summed over each
     group. Unknown means no matched mayor, or a winner with no matched
     party.
   - One regression on all three terms, with `S_p` and the baseline.
   - Report `beta_aligned`, `beta_unaligned`, `diff` (aligned minus
     unaligned), and `ri_p_aligned`, `ri_p_unaligned` and `ri_p_diff`, from
     the same draws.
   - Report `n_pairs_aligned`, `n_pairs_unaligned` and `n_pairs_unknown`.

### `results.json` keys (exactly these; no key contains a dot)

- `estimates.primary.w050`, `estimates.primary.w025`,
  `estimates.primary.w100`: each `{beta, ri_p, clustered_se, effect_sd,
  sd_pre, mean_abs_d, n_places, n_pairs}`.
- `estimates.falsification.w050`: the same keys.
- `estimates.secondary.lights`, `.builtup`, `.native`: each `{beta, ri_p,
  clustered_se, bh_p, bh_reject_05, mean_abs_d, n_places, n_pairs}`.
- `estimates.heterogeneity`: `{beta_aligned, beta_unaligned, diff,
  ri_p_aligned, ri_p_unaligned, ri_p_diff, n_pairs_aligned,
  n_pairs_unaligned, n_pairs_unknown, n_places}`.
- `estimates.finite` (bool): true only if every β, p, SE, `effect_sd`,
  `sd_pre` and `bh_p` above is a finite number. **Read by k1.**
- `design`: windows, draws, seed, elections, and the outcome years per
  outcome.

`summary.md` (at most 20 KB): one table per block, the primary first, and
the falsification beside it. Make no causal claim beyond the exploration
half. Say that a result here is a reason to register, not a finding.
