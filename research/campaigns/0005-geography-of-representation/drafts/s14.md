# Task: feasibility of the geography-of-representation design on pre-election years (explore, idea 0006)

## Goal

A new explore script, `explore/0006_feasibility.py`. Using **pre-election
outcome years only**, it measures whether idea 0006's design can work:

- enough close pairs;
- polling places located well enough;
- enough polling places matched to schools;
- baselines balanced on the treatment;
- a flat pre-election placebo;
- enough power.

Its `results.json` carries the six fields that step s15's kill rules read.
Campaign `research/campaigns/0005-geography-of-representation/campaign.md`,
step s14. Step s15 runs it through `explore-run`.

## Idea

Idea 0006 (`research/ideas/0006-geography-of-representation.md`), decision
0011 (national scope), decision 0003 (outcomes split), decision 0007's MDE
method (2.8 × the RI standard deviation of the slope). This is an **explore**
task (label `explore`): it may change only the two files below.

### Rules that bind this script

- **Exploration half, national.** Call
  `exploration_municipalities(store, national=True)` and `restrict` every
  table on `cd_ibge` before computing anything
  (`tests/test_explore.py` checks both).
- **Outcome years (referee R2).** For election E it reads `place_outcomes`
  files `place_outcomes_E<E>_y<y>.parquet` only for:
  - `y` = E-4 and E (school counts);
  - `y` = E (built-up share);
  - `y` in E-4 .. E-1 (night lights).
  
  It reads **no** `y` > E and never the column `native_10km`. The reader
  refuses (raises) any `y` > E. Treatment tables (`section_votes`,
  `tse_results`, `tse_parties`, `polling_places`, `school_polling_match`)
  are read for the elections 2008, 2012, 2016 and 2020.
- **Nothing beyond this issue (referee R7):** no post-election quantity, no
  estimate of the effect, no verdict on the kill rules. Do not import
  `terrain.registered`.
- **Data access:** read paths from the manifests in `data/manifests/build/`,
  as `explore/0005_enforcement_power.py` does. The national
  `section_votes` is large (SP 2020 alone is several million rows). Read it
  one state-year at a time with `pl.scan_parquet`, filtering `cd_cargo` 13,
  `nr_turno` 1 and the exploration `cd_ibge` before `collect()`.
- **Compute:** no package beyond what is installed (`numpy`, `polars`; there
  is no `scipy` or `pyfixest`). The run must finish well inside
  `explore-run`'s 180 minutes.

### Definitions (fixed; nothing else is computed)

- **Close pairs**, per election E in 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, from
  `terrain.strongholds`: `main_election`, `candidacy_votes`,
  `within_list_pairs`, `valid_votes`, `margins`, then
  `close_pairs(paired, threshold)` with threshold 0.005. The margin is the
  vote difference over the municipality's valid votes.
- **Shares at a place:** `place_shares(place_candidate_votes(section_votes,
  main))`; `s_cp` is the candidate's share of the place's valid (candidate
  plus legend) council votes. A candidate with no row at a place has share 0.
- **Treatment and control**, for each place p of a municipality with at
  least one close pair (sum over the municipality's close pairs k):
  - `D_p` = Σ_k (s_{w_k p} − s_{l_k p});
  - `S_p` = Σ_k (s_{w_k p} + s_{l_k p}).
- **Regression.** Outcome on `D_p`, `S_p`, the stated baseline and
  municipality-by-election fixed effects. The slope comes by Frisch-Waugh:
  1. demean every variable within municipality × election;
  2. partial the demeaned controls out of the demeaned `D` by least
     squares;
  3. β = Σ D̃ Ỹ / Σ D̃².
- **Randomisation inference:** 2,000 draws, seed 20261110. Each draw flips
  the winner of every close pair independently with probability 1/2, so
  `D_p` becomes Σ_k σ_k (s_{w_k p} − s_{l_k p}) with σ_k = ±1, and `S_p`
  stays fixed. Build D for all draws at once from (place, pair, difference)
  triples with `np.bincount`, in batches of draws. The p-value is `ri_p` of
  `explore/0001_power.py` (two-sided, (1 + #extreme) / (1 + draws)) and the
  spread is its `mde_stats` (loaded with `importlib`, as
  `explore/0005_enforcement_power.py` loads `0001_window.py`; not copied).
  The municipality-clustered SE is `clustered_se` of
  `explore/0001_placebo.py`.
- **Samples:**
  - *municipal-school places*: polling places of E whose `place_outcomes`
    row has a `school_id` and `school_rede` "3";
  - *located places*: rows with `located_1km` true.

### `results.json` keys (exactly these; no key contains a dot)

- `counts.pairs_050` (int): close pairs at 0.50%, pooled over the four
  elections. **Read by k1.**
- `counts.pairs_by_election` (object E → int).
- `counts.places_with_pairs` (int): places in municipalities with at least
  one close pair.
- `coverage.located_share` (float): the share of all sections of the
  exploration half (council, round 1, main election, distinct `ano_eleicao`,
  `sg_ue`, `nr_zona`, `nr_secao`, pooled over the four elections) whose
  polling place has a coordinate with `conf_dist_km` ≤ 1. **Read by k2.**
- `schools.match_share` (float): the share of the exploration half's
  polling places (pooled over the four elections) matched to a school at
  `high` or `medium` confidence in `school_polling_match`. **Read by k3.**
- `schools.municipal_places` (int), `schools.state_places` (int): places of
  each network, pooled.
- `balance.ri_p_joint` (float). **Read by k4.** Three baselines, each
  regressed on D with `S_p` and fixed effects:
  - the municipal-school facility count at y = E (municipal-school places,
    2008-2020);
  - the mean of `lights_1km` over the years E-4 .. E-1 that exist (located
    places; elections 2016 and 2020 only, since VIIRS starts in 2013, so
    2016 uses 2013-2015);
  - `builtup_1km` at y = E (located places, 2008-2020).
  
  With the same 2,000 draws for all three, T = Σ_j (β_j / sd_RI(β_j))², and
  each draw's T uses the same denominators. `ri_p_joint` is (1 + #{T_draw ≥
  T_obs}) / (1 + 2000). Also write `balance.beta` and `balance.ri_p`
  (objects with keys `school`, `lights`, `builtup`).
- `placebo.ri_p_school` (float). **Read by k5.**
  - Outcome: `school_n_fac` at E minus at E-4.
  - Controls: `S_p` and `school_n_fac` at E-4.
  - Sample: municipal-school places with both counts, elections 2012, 2016
    and 2020. 2008 is left out because INEP's data start in 2007, so there
    is no 2004.
  
  Also write `placebo.beta`, `placebo.clustered_se`, `placebo.n_places`.
- `power.mde_sd_school` (float). **Read by k6.**
  - It is 2.8 × sd_RI(β) of the placebo regression × mean |D_p| (over
    placebo-sample places with D_p ≠ 0), divided by the standard deviation
    (ddof 1) of the placebo outcome in its sample.
  - It is the minimum detectable effect at 80% power (5%, two-sided) of a
    mean-|D| shift, in standard deviations of the pre-election change.
  
  Also write `power.sd_ri`, `power.mean_abs_d`, `power.sd_pre_change`.
- `by_state` (object UF → object), with keys `pairs_050`, `located_share`,
  `match_share`, `ri_p_joint`, `ri_p_school` and `mde_sd_school`. These are
  the same quantities on the state's places, with the same draws. A value is
  `null` when its sample has fewer than 2 close pairs or no variation.
- `design`: `{"window": 0.005, "draws": 2000, "seed": 20261110,
  "elections": [2008, 2012, 2016, 2020], "outcome_years": "see the issue"}`.

`summary.md` (at most 20 KB) reports the pooled values and a by-state table,
with no statement about effects.

## Done when

1. The script calls `exploration_municipalities(store, national=True)` and
   `restrict` (`tests/test_explore.py` passes, including its national-idea
   check).
2. A test builds a small world in memory: two municipalities, each with a
   close pair and three places with known shares. It checks:
   - `D_p` and `S_p` against hand-computed values;
   - that flipping a pair flips the sign of its contribution;
   - that β from the Frisch-Waugh routine equals the β of a dense
     least-squares fit with dummies, to 1e-10.
3. With a planted balance violation (baseline = D), `balance.ri_p_joint` is
   small. With a baseline unrelated to D, it is not small (fixed seed).
4. The reader raises for any `y` > E, and the script never requests one
   (test with a fake store that records requested paths).
5. A test runs the full logic on the small world and checks that
   `results.json` has every key listed above, with `counts.pairs_050`,
   `coverage.located_share`, `schools.match_share`, `balance.ri_p_joint`,
   `placebo.ri_p_school` and `power.mde_sd_school` as numbers, and no key
   containing a dot.
6. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
