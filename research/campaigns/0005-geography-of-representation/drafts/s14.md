# Task: build the sealed place outcomes (build, idea 0006)

## Goal

A build, `place_outcomes`, that holds every outcome of idea 0006 per
polling place, election and outcome year. It is split by half like
`cell_outcomes` (decision 0003):

- exploration-half rows go to `data/derived/place_outcomes/`;
- confirmation-half rows go only to `sealed/outcomes/place_outcomes/`,
  which this build creates in and never reads.

Campaign `research/campaigns/0005-geography-of-representation/campaign.md`,
step s14. Step s15 runs it through `data-pipeline`.

## Idea

Idea 0006 (`research/ideas/0006-geography-of-representation.md`); decisions
0003 and 0011. The models are `src/terrain/builds/cell_outcomes.py` and
`src/terrain/builds/cell_enforcement.py`: the split by half, the
municipality polygons, and notes that count exploration rows only. The
inputs, by catalog name and the step that makes them:

- `polling_places` (national; s1 and s4);
- `school_polling_match` (s9, s10);
- `inep_schools` (s6, s7);
- `viirs_annual` and `mapbiomas_coverage_national` (s11, s12, s13);
- `ibge_boundaries_2024` (existing).

Their columns are in their contracts and catalog notes. This is a **build**
task: it may change exactly the files below.

## In scope

- `data/catalog.yaml` (modify): in the existing `place_outcomes` entry, add
  `contract: data/contracts/place_outcomes.md` and complete the notes.
  Never change its `name`, `derived` or `sealed` lines: they are pinned in
  `tests/test_split.py`.
- `src/terrain/builds/place_outcomes.py` (new).
- `pipeline/build/place_outcomes.py` (new): thin, as
  `pipeline/build/cell_outcomes.py`.
- `data/contracts/place_outcomes.md` (new).
- `tests/test_build_place_outcomes.py` (new).

Nothing else. `tests/test_split.py`, `src/terrain/explore.py`,
`pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock` are not touched. `tifffile`, `pyproj`,
`shapely`, `pyshp`, `numpy` and `polars` are already installed.

### Inputs

- The rasters: one GeoTIFF per year on the grid of the catalog's
  `grid_km` (s11's entries).
  - `mapbiomas_coverage_national` has bands `urban` and `native` (shares ×
    10000).
  - `viirs_annual` has one band, `log(1 + radiance)` × 1000, for 2013-2024.
- `ibge_boundaries_2024`: `BR_Municipios_2024.zip`, read as
  `cell_enforcement.py` does.
- Both exploration lists: `terrain.explore.EXPLORE_LISTS_NATIONAL`, parsed
  with `cell_outcomes.parse_split_list`. A code in both lists stops the
  build.

### Rows and files

One row per (election `E` in 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020; polling place of `E`;
outcome year `y`), for every `y` in E-4 .. E+4 that has any source. Files
are one Parquet per (E, y): `place_outcomes_E<E>_y<y>.parquet`, so an
explore script can read exactly the years it states (referee R2).

### Columns

- The polling-place key (`ano` = E, `cd_ibge`, `sg_ue`, `nr_zona`,
  `nr_local_votacao`) and `y`.
- `located_1km`: the place has a coordinate with `conf_dist_km` ≤ 1.
- **Schools**, from the match of year E at high or medium confidence only:
  - `school_id`: null if there is no such match;
  - `school_rede`: from `inep_schools` at year E ("3" municipal, "2" state);
  - `school_n_fac`: how many of these nine facilities the school has at
    year `y`:
    - `agua_rede_publica`, `esgoto_rede_publica`, `energia_rede_publica`,
      `internet`, `biblioteca_sala_leitura`, `laboratorio_ciencias`,
      `laboratorio_informatica`, `quadra_esportes`;
    - accessibility: `dependencia_pne` for `y` ≤ 2018, and
      `1 - acessibilidade_inexistente` for `y` ≥ 2019 (INEP changed the
      question in 2019).
  
  The count is 0-9. It is null if the school has no row in `y` or any of the
  nine is null; count those nulls per year in the notes.
- **Rings.** A cell is in a place's ring when its centre lies within the
  radius of the place's point (Albers km) **and** inside the polygon of the
  place's own municipality. A ring therefore never reads a cell of a
  neighbouring municipality, which may be in the other half. Only places
  with `located_1km` get ring values; the rest are null.
  - `lights_1km`: the mean of the VIIRS value / 1000 over the 1 km ring;
    null for years without a VIIRS file.
  - `builtup_1km`: the mean of `urban` / 10000 over the 1 km ring.
  - `native_10km`: the mean of `native` / 10000 over the 10 km ring.
  - `ring_cells_1km`, `ring_cells_10km`: the number of cells.
  
  A ring with no cell (rare at 1 km) gives null, and is counted.

### Split, notes and path

- **Split:** rows whose `cd_ibge` is in the exploration lists go to the
  derived file. All others go to the sealed file, including municipalities
  in neither list. Upload with create-only (`cell_outcomes._upload`); the
  sealed folder is never listed or read.
- **Notes:** counts of exploration-half rows only, per (E, y):
  - places, and places with a school, by `school_rede`;
  - null `school_n_fac`;
  - located places and empty rings.
  
  Never a pooled count, never a mean or other statistic of any outcome.
  Error messages follow the same rule.
- **Output path:** `content_hash` of the input SHA-256s, the SHA-256 of
  both exploration lists, the years, and the code of the build and of every
  `terrain` module it imports.
- **Memory:** read one raster year at a time. Find each place's 10 km ring
  cells once (about 314 cells), as integer offsets into the year's array.

## Out of scope

- Any statistic of an outcome (means, regressions, comparisons): that is
  the explore steps.
- Reading `sealed/` or `confirm/`; changing `tests/test_split.py`,
  `terrain.explore` or any acquirer.

## Done when

1. The `place_outcomes` entry keeps its `name`, `derived` and `sealed`
   lines, and `tests/test_split.py` passes unchanged.
2. On inputs built in the test, with two municipalities, one in each
   exploration list, and one in neither:
   - rows of the two exploration municipalities are in the derived files;
     all other rows are only in the sealed files;
   - a 10 km ring at a municipal border excludes the neighbour's cells;
   - `school_n_fac` switches its accessibility source between 2018 and
     2019;
   - a null facility gives a null count;
   - low-confidence matches give no school;
   - the notes hold no value derived from a confirmation-half row.
3. A code in both exploration lists, or a missing list, stops the build
   before anything is uploaded.
4. `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `uv run pytest`
   pass.
