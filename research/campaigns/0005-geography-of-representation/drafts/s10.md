# Task: export night lights and MapBiomas coverage for Brazil, and build the sealed place outcomes (build, idea 0006)

## Goal

Three datasets for idea 0006:

- `viirs_annual`: VIIRS night lights per 1 km cell of Brazil and year;
- `mapbiomas_coverage_national`: each 1 km cell's urban share and native
  vegetation share per year;
- `place_outcomes`: every outcome of idea 0006 per polling place, election
  and outcome year.

`place_outcomes` is split by half like `cell_outcomes` (decision 0003).
Exploration-half rows go to `data/derived/place_outcomes/`, and
confirmation-half rows only to `sealed/outcomes/place_outcomes/`, which this
build creates in and never reads. Campaign
`research/campaigns/0005-geography-of-representation/campaign.md`, step s10.
Steps s11 and s12 run the two exports and s13 the build, through
`data-pipeline`.

## Idea

Idea 0006 (`research/ideas/0006-geography-of-representation.md`);
decisions 0003 and 0011. The models are:

- `src/terrain/earthengine.py` (the MapBiomas export of

- `src/terrain/builds/cell_outcomes.py` and
  `src/terrain/builds/cell_enforcement.py` (the split by half, the

### `earthengine.py`: three export modes

The block of `mapbiomas_deforestation` must give byte-for-byte the same
export and the same `source` etag as today: do not add keys to it. New
optional keys, all read in `export_params`:

- `mode`: `shares` (the default, today's behaviour: one band per code),
  `groups`, or `log1p_mean`.
- `groups` (mode `groups`): a mapping from band name to a list of codes.
  Each band is the share of 30 m pixels of the cell whose code is in the
  list, times `scale`, as `uint16`.
- mode `log1p_mean`: `collections`, a list of `{asset, years}` (each year
  taken from exactly one collection), and `band`. Each year is the mean,
  over the cell, of `log(1 + max(band, 0))` of the image whose start date is
  in that year, times `scale`, as `uint16`. That is one band per file. A year
  with no image, or with several, fails the run before any export starts.
  The manifest records each year's image id.
- `file_dimensions` (default `[3584, 2816]`) and `min_share_with_data`
  (default 0.5): the national grid needs larger files and has more empty
  cells.
- `check_file` per mode:
  - `shares` and `groups`: today's sum check;
  - `log1p_mean`: no sum check, but every value must be at most
    `11 * scale` (a log radiance of 11 means about 60,000 nW/sr/cm²,
    beyond any real pixel).
  
  The share of cells with data (any band above 0) must be at least
  `min_share_with_data` in every mode.

### Catalog: `mapbiomas_coverage_national`

- `source`: "MapBiomas Brasil Collection 10, land use and land cover
  (coverage), Earth Engine asset below".
- `licence`: "CC BY 4.0 (MapBiomas: Earth Engine catalog, the MapBiomas
  site and its Dataverse); cite as 'MapBiomas – Coleção 10 da série anual
  de Mapas de Cobertura e Uso da Terra do Brasil, acessado em <date> através
  do link: <link>'". Note: the older `mapbiomas_deforestation` entry says
  CC-BY-SA 4.0; this entry states what MapBiomas states today.
- `gcs: data/raw/mapbiomas/coverage/`; `status: planned`.
- `earthengine:`:
  - `asset: projects/mapbiomas-public/assets/brazil/lulc/collection10/mapbiomas_brazil_collection10_coverage_v2`
    (listed on MapBiomas' tools page beside the deforestation asset;
    Wayback snapshots of 2025-10-09 and 2026-06-23);
  - `band: classification_<year>` and `mode: groups`;
  - `groups`:
    - `urban: [24]` (Área Urbanizada);
    - `native: [3, 4, 5, 6, 49, 11, 12, 32, 29, 50]` (the vegetation
      classes of level 1 "Floresta" and "Vegetação Herbácea e Arbustiva"
      in the Collection 10 legend, all marked natural cover in its ATBD,
      Table 3);
  - `years`: 2004 to 2024, every year; `scale: 10000`;
  - `grid_km: {x0: 2813, x1: 7400, y0: 7600, y1: 11912}`: Brazil in the
    project's Albers km, from its extreme points (west 2822, east 7384 at
    Fernando de Noronha, south 7614, north 11903) plus 10 km;
  - `file_dimensions: [4608, 4352]`; `min_share_with_data: 0.35` (Brazil is
    about 43% of that box);
  - `file: coverage_shares_<year>.tif`.
  
  If a band name does not exist in the asset, the run fails before any
  export (check the asset's band names first).
- `notes`: what the bands mean, that "native" is MapBiomas' natural
  vegetation (water, beaches and rock outcrops excluded except code 29,
  which is a natural non-forest class), the extent, and that the file holds
  outcome data for both halves.

### Catalog: `viirs_annual`

- `source`: "NOAA / Earth Observation Group, VIIRS Nighttime Lights
  annual composites (VNL V2.1 and V2.2), Earth Engine collections below".
- `licence`: "Public domain per the Earth Engine catalog ('not subject to
  copyright and carry no restrictions'); EOG states that many of its VIIRS
  Nighttime Lights data are under CC BY 4.0. Cite Elvidge et al. (2021),
  Remote Sensing 13(5), 922, and EOG as the source."
- `gcs: data/raw/viirs/annual/`; `status: planned`.
- `earthengine:`:
  - `mode: log1p_mean`; `band: average_masked` (EOG: "average monthly
    radiance with background masked"; 463.83 m pixels);
  - `collections`:
    - `{asset: NOAA/VIIRS/DNB/ANNUAL_V21, years: [2013, 2014, 2015, 2016,
      2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021]}`;
    - `{asset: NOAA/VIIRS/DNB/ANNUAL_V22, years: [2022, 2023, 2024]}`;
  - `scale: 1000`; the same `grid_km` and `file_dimensions` as above;
    `min_share_with_data: 0.01`;
  - `file: viirs_log1p_<year>.tif`.
- Why these years:
  - V2.1's text says it covers 2013-2021 and that 2012 was processed
    differently and is excluded.
  - V2.2's text says 2022, and its catalog availability now runs to 2024.
  - Whether 2023 and 2024 exist is unconfirmed, so the run checks that each
    listed year has exactly one image.
  - EOG documents a method change between V2.1 and V2.2 (mixed satellites
    in 2022; months with more than one cloud-free observation kept). Put
    this in `notes`.
  
  **There is no 2012 image.** Idea 0006's lights outcome for the 2016
  election therefore uses the pre-election years that exist, 2013-2015
  (campaign file, "Steps and why").

### `place_outcomes`

**Inputs:**

- the national `polling_places` (s4);
- `school_polling_match` (s9);
- `inep_schools` (s7);
- `viirs_annual` (s11);
- `mapbiomas_coverage_national` (s12);
- `ibge_boundaries_2024` (`BR_Municipios_2024.zip`, read as
  `cell_enforcement.py` does);
- both exploration lists: `terrain.explore.EXPLORE_LISTS_NATIONAL`, parsed
  with `cell_outcomes.parse_split_list`. A code in both lists stops the
  build.

**Rows:** one per (election `E` in 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020; polling place of
`E`; outcome year `y`), for every `y` in E-4 .. E+4 that has any source.
Files are one Parquet per (E, y): `place_outcomes_E<E>_y<y>.parquet`. An
explore script can then read exactly the years it states (referee R2).

**Columns:**

- The polling-place key (`ano` = E, `cd_ibge`, `sg_ue`, `nr_zona`,
  `nr_local_votacao`) and `y`.
- `located_1km`: the place has a coordinate with `conf_dist_km` ≤ 1.
- **Schools.** From the s9 match of year E, at high or medium confidence
  only:
  - `school_id` (null if none), and `school_rede` (from `inep_schools` at
    year E; "3" municipal, "2" state);
  - `school_n_fac`: the number of these nine facilities at year `y`:
    `agua_rede_publica`, `esgoto_rede_publica`, `energia_rede_publica`,
    `internet`, `biblioteca_sala_leitura`, `laboratorio_ciencias`,
    `laboratorio_informatica`, `quadra_esportes`, and accessibility.
    Accessibility is `dependencia_pne` for `y` ≤ 2018 and
    `1 - acessibilidade_inexistente` for `y` ≥ 2019 (INEP changed the
    question in 2019).
  - The count is 0-9, and null if the school has no row in `y` or any of
    the nine is null. Count those nulls per year in the notes.
- **Rings.** A cell is in a place's ring when its centre lies within the
  radius of the place's point (Albers km) **and** inside the polygon of the
  place's own municipality. So a ring never reads a cell of a neighbouring
  municipality, which may be in the other half. Only places with
  `located_1km` get ring values; the rest are null.
  - `lights_1km`: the mean of `viirs_log1p` / 1000 over the 1 km ring;
    null for years without a VIIRS file.
  - `builtup_1km`: the mean of `urban` / 10000 over the 1 km ring.
  - `native_10km`: the mean of `native` / 10000 over the 10 km ring.
  - `ring_cells_1km`, `ring_cells_10km`: the number of cells.
  
  A ring with no cell (rare at 1 km) gives null and is counted.

**Split:** rows whose `cd_ibge` is in the exploration lists go to the
derived file; all others (including municipalities in neither list) go to
the sealed file. Upload with create-only (`cell_outcomes._upload`); the
sealed folder is never listed or read.

**Notes:** counts of exploration-half rows only, per (E, y): places,
places with a school by `school_rede`, null `school_n_fac`, located places,
and empty rings. Never a pooled count, never a mean or other statistic of
any outcome. Error messages follow the same rule.

**Output path:** `content_hash` of the input SHA-256s, the SHA-256 of both
exploration lists, the years and the code of the build and of every
`terrain` module it imports.

**Memory:** read one raster year at a time. Read the 10 km ring cells once
per place (about 314 cells), as integer offsets into the year's array.

4. `place_outcomes` on inputs built in the test (two municipalities, one in

   - rows of the two exploration municipalities are in the derived files;
     all other rows are only in the sealed files;
   - a 10 km ring at a municipal border excludes the neighbour's cells;
   - `school_n_fac` switches accessibility source between 2018 and 2019;
   - a null facility gives a null count;
   - low-confidence matches give no school;
   - the notes hold no value derived from a confirmation-half row.

   before anything is uploaded.

   pass.
